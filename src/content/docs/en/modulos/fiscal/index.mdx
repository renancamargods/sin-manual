## Overview

The **Tax** module brings together the tax settings that guide how the system calculates and applies taxes in commercial operations.

## When to use this module

- Look up or adjust registered tax types.
- Track the sequence in which tax rules are applied.
- Enable specific features of the tax module.

## How to access

Side menu → Modules → Tax

<Callout type="cuidado" title="Tax impact">
Changes to the tax settings may affect the tax calculation in orders and invoices that have already been issued.
</Callout>
