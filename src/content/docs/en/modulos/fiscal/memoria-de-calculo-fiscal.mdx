## Overview

The **Tax Calculation Memory** screen queries the **record of the tax calculation** performed for orders. The calculation runs in the background and stores the "memory" (how the tax was computed). Here you can search that record by order/customer, see the **execution type** (Simulation, Shadow or Active) and the **comparison result** (whether it matched, diverged or failed).

## When to use this screen

- Check how the tax calculation was computed on an order.
- Find orders with a **divergent** or **error** calculation.
- Compare simulation/shadow executions with the active calculation.

## How to access

Tax → Tax Calculation Memory

<Callout type="info" title="Execution types and comparison">
**Execution type** — *Simulation* (test calculation), *Shadow* (runs in parallel, not binding) and *Active* (the calculation that counts).
**Comparison** — *Matched*, *Divergent* (difference to investigate), *Error* (failed) and *Not compared*.
</Callout>

## Main flows

1. **Query** — enter the filters (order, customer, execution type, comparison, period) and click **"Search"**.
2. **Analyze** — see the calculation memory and comparison result in the **Results**.
3. **Clear** — use **"Clear Filters"** to redo the search.

## What this screen does not do

- It does not recalculate the tax — it only **queries** the record of the calculation already done.
