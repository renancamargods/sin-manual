## Overview

The **Simulator** module gathers **step-by-step guided trainings** of the system screens. Each training is a practice space where you walk a routine from start to finish — from pre-registration to completion — with **Dentinho** explaining each stage and showing the **why** behind every block.

## When to use this module

- Learn a screen hands-on, with no fear of mistakes.
- Train a new user before they touch the real system.
- Understand the rules (required fields, approval levels, statuses) interactively.

## How to access

Side menu → Modules → Simulator

<Callout type="dica" title="🦷 It's just training">
Nothing you do in the simulator is saved in the system. It's a practice space — click freely, go back and restart as many times as you want.
</Callout>
