## Overview

The **Contract** screen brings together the registration of commercial contracts signed with customers, including validity, expected consumption targets and specific policies applied to the contract.

## When to use this screen

- Register a new contract.
- Look up data and validity of an existing contract.
- Change terms or attach documents to the contract.
- Track consumption targets linked to the contract.

## How to access

Contracts → Contract

## Before you start

- Confirm the customer is already registered in the Customers module.
- Have at hand the commercial terms and the agreed validity.
- Check whether there is a specific contract policy to apply.

## Main flows

1. **Register new contract** — select the customer, set the validity and save.
2. **Look up contract** — use the search to find it by customer or code.
3. **Change contract** — update terms, validity or attachments.
4. **End contract** — change the status when the contract reaches its end.

## Relationships with other records or processes

This record depends on a customer already registered in the Customers module. Consumption targets and contract policies are linked to this record.

## Important parameters and behaviors

- The contract must be active to be considered in linked targets and policies.
- Changes to the validity apply from the informed date.
- Ending a contract does not delete the history of targets already recorded.

## Rules and cautions

- Review customer, validity and terms before saving the contract.
- Avoid registering duplicate contracts for the same customer and period.
- Prefer to end rather than delete a contract that already has linked movements.
- Check your permissions before changing an active contract.
