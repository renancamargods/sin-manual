## Overview

The **Contract Policy** screen gathers the **commercial rules** applied to the contracts (the "packages"). Each policy has a **title**, a **classification**, a **validity**, a **rebate** and the **line of business**, plus tabs for **consumption volumes**, **discounts** and **loaned items**.

## When to use this screen

- Register a contract policy (package).
- View the policies already registered.
- Adjust rebate, discounts, consumption volumes or loaned items of a policy.

## How to access

Contracts → Contract Policy

## Before you start

- Define the **title** and the **classification** of the contract.
- Have the **validity (in months)**, the **rebate %** and the **line of business**.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the filters and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register** — click **"+ New"**, choose "Contract Policy" or "Course Contract Policy", fill in the tabs and click **"Save"**.
3. **Edit** — use the **Edit** icon on the row, adjust the tabs and click **"Save"**.
4. **View details** — use the **Details** icon.

## Relationship with other processes

- **Contract** — the policies define the commercial rules applied to the contracts.
- **Consumption Target** — deals with the targets set in a contract (complementary to the policy volumes).

## What this screen does not do

- It does not sign the contract — it defines the rules (policies) that will be applied to it.
