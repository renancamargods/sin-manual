## Overview

The **Contracts** module brings together the registration and tracking of commercial contracts signed with customers, including consumption targets and specific policies applied to each contract.

## When to use this module

- Register a new contract.
- Track expected consumption targets.
- Look up attachments and policies linked to the contract.

## How to access

Side menu → Modules → Contracts

<Callout type="info" title="Relationship with customers">
A contract is always linked to a customer registered in the Customers module.
</Callout>
