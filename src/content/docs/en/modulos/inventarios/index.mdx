## Overview

The **Inventories** module organizes stock counting, allowing you to open an inventory, record counts and track which inventories are available for checking.

## When to use this module

- Open a new inventory.
- Record the count of items.
- Look up inventories available for counting.

## How to access

Side menu → Modules → Inventories

<Callout type="cuidado" title="Impact on stock">
The counts recorded here may generate adjustments to the products’ stock balance.
</Callout>
