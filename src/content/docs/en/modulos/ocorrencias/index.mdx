## Overview

The **Occurrences** module brings together the recording of occurrences related to product, supplier, customer or quality, as well as satisfaction surveys and practical training requests.

## When to use this module

- Register a new occurrence.
- Classify the occurrence by incidence type.
- Track surveys and hands-on requests.

## How to access

Side menu → Modules → Occurrences

<Callout type="info" title="Routing">
Each occurrence may involve different areas of the company, depending on the incidence type selected.
</Callout>
