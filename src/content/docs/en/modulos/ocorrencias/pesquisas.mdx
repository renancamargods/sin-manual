## Overview

The **Surveys** screen is a **survey (questionnaire) builder**. Each survey has a **title**, a **slug** (unique identifier generated from the title) and the **Active** flag, plus **sections** that organize the questions. The screen also lets you check the **answers** and, from an answer, **generate a PFCQ Occurrence**.

## When to use this screen

- Create a new survey (questionnaire).
- Add sections and questions to a survey.
- Check the answers of a survey.
- Generate a PFCQ Occurrence from an answer.

## How to access

Occurrences → Surveys

## Main flows

1. **Create** — click **"+ New"**, enter the "Title", adjust the "Slug" and mark "Active".
2. **Build** — in "Sections", click **"+ New section"** and add the questions.
3. **Save** — click **"Save"**.
4. **Check answers** — open the **"Answers"** tab.
5. **Generate occurrence** — use the **"Generate PFCQ Occurrence"** button to open an occurrence from the survey/answer.

## Relationship with other processes

- **PFCQ Occurrences** — the "Generate PFCQ Occurrence" button creates an occurrence from the survey.

## What this screen does not do

- It does not handle the occurrence — it only generates it; handling happens on the PFCQ Occurrences screen.
