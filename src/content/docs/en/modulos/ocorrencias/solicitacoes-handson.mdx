## Overview

The **Hands On Requests** screen gathers the recording and tracking of **hands-on training requests (HandsOn)**. Each request stores the **customer**, the **consultant**, the **type** and the **reason** of the occurrence, the **course and speaker** data and the **travel logistics** (institution, hotel, dates and airport), plus the **status** of the occurrence.

## When to use this screen

- Record a new HandsOn request.
- View and track open requests.
- Organize the logistics (speaker, dates, hotel and airport) of a training.

## How to access

Occurrences → Hands On Requests

The **"Occurrence Products"** tab registers the products related to the request.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the filters and click **"Search"**.
2. **Record** — click **"+ New"**, fill in the course data and the logistics and click **"Save"**.
3. **Edit** — use the **Edit** icon on the row, adjust the fields and click **"Save"**.
