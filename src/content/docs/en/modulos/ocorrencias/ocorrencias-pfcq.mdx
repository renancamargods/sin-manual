## Overview

The **PFCQ Occurrences** screen gathers the recording and tracking of **occurrences** (related to product, supplier, customer or quality). Each occurrence stores the **customer data**, the **products** involved, the **attachments**, the **activities** and a **status flow** that tracks the progress. Occurrences can be linked to a **CAPA Number** and a **Ticket Number**.

## When to use this screen

- Record a new occurrence.
- View and track open occurrences.
- Track the status flow and activities of an occurrence.

## How to access

Occurrences → PFCQ Occurrences

## Before you start

- Know the **customer** related to the occurrence.
- Have, when available, the **CAPA Number** and the **Ticket Number**.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the filters and click **"Search"**.
2. **Record** — click **"+ New"**, fill in the data, the products and the activities and click **"Save"**.
3. **Track** — open the occurrence and check the "Status Flow" and the "Activities".
4. **Edit** — use the **Edit** icon on the row, adjust the tabs and click **"Save"**.
