## Overview

The **Ignition** module brings together panels with consolidated indicators for day-to-day operational tracking.

## When to use this module

- Track consolidated indicators from other areas of the system.
- Monitor the progress of processes in real time.

## How to access

Side menu → Modules → Ignition

<Callout type="info" title="Consolidated content">
The indicators shown here reflect information registered in other modules of the system.
</Callout>
