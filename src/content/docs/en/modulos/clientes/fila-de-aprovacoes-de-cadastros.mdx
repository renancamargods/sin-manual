## Overview

The **Registration Approval Queue** screen (whose header appears as **"Approval Queue - Customers"**) gathers the **customer registrations awaiting approval**. From it, the responsible team filters the pending registrations, tracks the totalizers and handles the customers that are **to release** or **under evaluation**.

## When to use this screen

- Track the customer registrations awaiting approval.
- Filter the registrations by person type (Individual, Company, Foreign) and by situation.
- See the customers to release and those under evaluation.
- Track the quantity of customers awaiting approval.

## How to access

Customers → Registration Approval Queue

## Before you start

- Know which registrations you want to track (customer, person type, company).
- Confirm that your profile takes part in the registration approval.

<Callout type="info" title="Relationship with the approval flows">
This queue is part of the **Customer Registration x Approvals** flows (with and without Ignition). The stages and approvers follow the configured approval rule.
</Callout>

## What the screen does not do

- It does not create the customer registration — it only handles those awaiting approval.
- It does not, by itself, define the approval rule — it comes from the Approvals module.
