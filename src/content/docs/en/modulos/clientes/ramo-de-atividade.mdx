## Overview

The **Line of Business** screen keeps the list of the customers' **lines of business** — for example, "Clinic", "Office", "Student", "Foreign Customer" and "Dental Distributor". The line of business helps to **classify** the customer and is used in other screens and in **contracts**.

## When to use this screen

- Query the registered lines of business.
- Add a new line of business.
- Fix the description of a line of business.

## How to access

Customers → Line of Business

## Before you start

- Define the line of business **description**.
- Confirm that your profile allows maintaining this registration.

## What the screen does not do

- It does not classify customers automatically — it only maintains the list.
- It does not define commercial rules by itself.
