## Overview

The **SIN Partner** screen keeps the **SIN Partner program partners** (identified by a **description**). Each partner can have its own **customer list**, allowing the customers linked to each partnership to be handled together.

## When to use this screen

- Query the registered SIN Partner partners.
- Create a new partner.
- Associate customers with a partner.

## How to access

Customers → SIN Partner

## Before you start

- Define the partner **description**.
- Know which **customers** belong to it, if applicable.
- Confirm that your profile allows maintaining this registration.

## What the screen does not do

- It does not register the customers — they come from the Customer registration.
- It does not define prices or discounts by itself.
