## Overview

The **Special Entity Parameter** screen defines **special handling** applied to a **customer** or **order**, by **type** (for example, "Registration Status", "Order Status", "Financial Information", "Bank Slips" and "Logistics Information") and by a **control period** (start and end). It is used to configure exceptions or differentiated handling in a controlled way.

## When to use this screen

- Apply special handling to a customer or order for a period.
- Query the registered special parameters.
- Activate or deactivate a parameter.

## How to access

Customers → Special Entity Parameter

## Before you start

- Know the **type** of special handling that will be configured.
- Have the **customer** and/or the **order** and the control **period**.
- Confirm that your profile allows maintaining this registration.

## What the screen does not do

- It does not create the customer or the order — they come from their own registrations.
- It is not a general rule — each parameter is specific and temporary.
