## Overview

The **Customers** module centralizes the commercial and tax records used to relate the company to its customers. The information registered here is used in orders, contracts, invoicing and commercial policies.

## When to use this module

- Register a new customer.
- Look up or change commercial and tax data.
- Organize customers into networks, groupings or classifications.
- Configure specific information for special customers.

## How to access

Side menu → Modules → Customers

<Callout type="cuidado" title="Tax data">
Review documents, addresses and tax information before using the customer record in orders or contracts.
</Callout>
