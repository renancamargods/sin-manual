## Overview

The **Economic Group** screen gathers the **customers that belong to the same economic group**. Each group has a **name**, a **purpose** (for example, "Credit") and a **status** (Active). The group is used to handle its customers together — for example, in credit tracking.

## When to use this screen

- Create an economic group to gather related customers.
- Query the registered economic groups.
- Activate or deactivate a group.
- Apply an economic group in bulk.

## How to access

Customers → Economic Group

## Before you start

- Define the group **name** and its **purpose** (e.g., "Credit").
- Know which **customers** will be part of the group.
- Confirm that your profile allows maintaining this registration.

## Main flows

1. **Create the group** — click **"+ New Economic Group"**, inform the name and purpose and use **"Save and Advance"**.
2. **Compose the group** — follow the steps to include the group customers.
3. **Apply in bulk** — when needed, use **"+ Apply Economic Group in Bulk"**.

## What the screen does not do

- It does not register the customers — they come from the Customer registration.
- It does not, by itself, define each customer's credit limit — it organizes the group.
