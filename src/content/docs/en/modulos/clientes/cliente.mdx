## Overview

The **Customer** screen is where customers are registered in the system, in the types **Individual (PF)**, **Company (PJ)** and **Foreign**. From it, the customer is filled in, saved and sent for review by the approval levels.

Registration starts with a **pre-registration** (integration with Pulse Credit) and moves on to the **full registration**, with the required data. After being saved, the customer can be sent for approval; when approved, it becomes **ACTIVE** and automatically receives the **SAP code**.

This screen is the starting point of the [Customer Creation and Approval Flow](/en/tecnico/fluxos/criacao-de-cliente).

## When to use this screen

- Register a new customer (individual, company or foreign).
- Look up or change a customer’s data.
- Send a customer for review by the approval levels.
- Track the registration status until activation.

## How to access

Customer Module → Customer Screen

## Before you start

- Have the customer’s document (CPF or CNPJ) and know the **person type** and the **registration type**.
- Gather the required data: name, address, phone, email and credential.
- Confirm that your profile (Master or Common) allows creating the customer.

## Main flows

1. **Create customer** — click **"+ New Customer"**, fill in the pre-registration and click **Next**; fill in the required data and click **Save** (or **Save and Continue**).
2. **Send for approval** — click **"Send Customer"** and confirm in the popup (**YES**). The customer goes to the approval queue according to the person type.
3. **Track** — the customer stays with the status **"Under Review by Registration/Credit"** until the level’s decision.

## What the screen does not do

- It does not approve the customer — the decision happens in the Registration Approvals Queue.
- It does not generate the SAP code directly — it is created when the customer is approved.
- It does not send the customer before saving — the "Send Customer" button is only enabled after saving.
