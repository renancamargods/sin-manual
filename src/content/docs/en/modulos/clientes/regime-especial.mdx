## Overview

The **Special Regime** screen keeps the **special (tax) regimes** applicable to customers. Each regime has a **code**, a **description**, a **validity period** (start and end) and a **status**, and can have its own **customer list**.

## When to use this screen

- Register a special regime and its validity.
- Query the registered special regimes.
- Associate customers with a special regime.
- Activate or deactivate a regime.

## How to access

Customers → Special Regime

## Before you start

- Have the **code** and the **description** of the regime.
- Define the **validity period** (start and end).
- Confirm that your profile allows maintaining this registration.

## What the screen does not do

- It does not register the customers — they come from the Customer registration.
- It does not change invoices already issued.
