## Overview

The **Stock Balance** screen shows the products' **stock position** by **Lounge** and by **warehouse**, with the available **quantity** and the product **visibility**. It is used to check how much of each product there is in each unit.

  cards={[

/>

## When to use this screen

- Query a product's stock balance.
- See the quantity by Lounge and by warehouse.
- Filter the products by visibility.

## How to access

Products → Stock Balance

## Before you start

- Know the **product** or the **Lounge** you want to query.
- Confirm that your profile has access to the screen.

## What the screen does not do

- It does not register the product — it comes from the Product registration.
- It is not a movement report — it shows the current position.
