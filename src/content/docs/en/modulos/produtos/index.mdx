## Overview

The **Products** module centralizes the registration of the products sold, including hierarchy, packaging, taxes, images and stock balance.

## When to use this module

- Register a new product.
- Look up or change product data.
- Organize products by family, brand or hierarchy.
- Look up the available stock balance.

## How to access

Side menu → Modules → Products

<Callout type="cuidado" title="Use in other modules">
The product record is used in orders, invoicing, regulatory and quality. Keep the information always up to date.
</Callout>
