## Overview

The **Product Tax** screen configures a **product's (SKU) tax percentage** by **country** — for example, "Portugal" with "23.00". It is used in taxation, especially in international operations, where the rate varies by country.

## When to use this screen

- Configure a product's tax percentage by country.
- Query the taxes by SKU or by country.
- Fix a product's rate in a country.

## How to access

Products → Product Tax

## Before you start

- Have the product **code (SKU)**, the **country** and the **tax percentage**.
- Confirm that your profile allows maintaining this registration.

## What the screen does not do

- It does not calculate the order tax — it only defines the percentage by product and country.
- It does not register the product — it comes from the Product registration.
