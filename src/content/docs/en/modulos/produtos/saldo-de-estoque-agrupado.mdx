## Overview

The **Grouped Stock Balance** screen shows the products' **stock position** in a **grouped (consolidated)** way, by **Lounge**, **product** and **description**, with the **quantity**. It is a summarized view, complementary to the **Stock Balance** (which details by warehouse).

## When to use this screen

- Query the stock balance in a consolidated way.
- See the grouped quantity by Lounge and product.

## How to access

Products → Grouped Stock Balance

## Before you start

- Know the **product** or the **Lounge** you want to query.
- Confirm that your profile has access to the screen.

## What the screen does not do

- It does not register the product — it comes from the Product registration.
- It does not adjust stock — it is a query.
