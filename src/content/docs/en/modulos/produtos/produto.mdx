## Overview

The **Product** screen centralizes the registration of the products sold by the company, including identification data, hierarchy, packaging and information used by other modules, such as regulatory and quality.

A complete product record is essential for orders, invoicing and regulatory controls to work correctly.

## When to use this screen

- Register a new product.
- Look up information about an already registered product.
- Change identification, hierarchy or packaging data.
- Look up the available stock balance for the product.

## How to access

Products → Product

## Before you start

- Confirm the correct family, group and hierarchy for the new product.
- Check whether the product is not already registered, to avoid duplicates.
- Have at hand the packaging information, barcode (GTIN) and images, if available.

## Main flows

1. **Register a new product** — fill in the required data and save the record.
2. **Look up a product** — use the search to find it by code or description.
3. **Change a product** — open the record, update the necessary fields and save.
4. **Look up stock balance** — access the available stock information for the product.

## Relationships with other records or processes

This record can be used in orders, invoicing, inventories, quality and regulatory records. Changes to the product hierarchy or classification may impact reports that use this grouping.

## Important parameters and behaviors

- The product must be active to be used in new orders.
- Changes to the record apply to new operations.
- Some fields may appear depending on the product’s regulatory line.

## Rules and cautions

- Review the data before saving the record.
- Avoid registering the same product more than once; use the search before creating a new record.
- Prefer to inactivate the product instead of deleting it when it is already used in orders or stock.
- Check your permissions before changing product data.
