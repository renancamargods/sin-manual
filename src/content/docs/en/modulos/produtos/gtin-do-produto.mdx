## Overview

The **Product GTIN** screen links the **GTIN barcodes** to the **products**. Each record relates a **GTIN** to a **product** (SKU), allowing the product to be identified by reading the barcode.

  cards={[

  ]}
/>

## When to use this screen

- Link a GTIN to a product.
- Query the registered GTINs by product or SKU.
- Fix the link between GTIN and product.

## How to access

Products → Product GTIN

## Before you start

- Have the **GTIN** (barcode) and the **product** (SKU) to link.
- Confirm that your profile allows maintaining this registration.

## What the screen does not do

- It does not register the product — it only links the GTIN to it.
- It does not generate the barcodes — it only records them.
