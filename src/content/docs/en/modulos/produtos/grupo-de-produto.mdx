## Overview

The **Product Group** screen organizes products into **groups** within a **family**. Each group has a **code**, a **description** and a **family**, and links to **products**, **consumption goals**, **contract and commercial policy items**, **campaign items** and **images**.

## When to use this screen

- Query the registered product groups.
- Create a new group within a family.
- Link the group to products, goals, policies and campaigns.

## How to access

Products → Product Group

## Before you start

- Define the group **code**, **description** and **family**.
- Confirm that your profile allows maintaining this registration.

## What the screen does not do

- It does not register the products — only the group and its links.
- It does not control stock.
