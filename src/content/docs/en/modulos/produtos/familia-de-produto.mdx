## Overview

The **Product Family** screen groups products by **family** (e.g., "Consumables", "Instruments", "Kits"). Besides the basic data (code, description, type), the family acts as a **linking point** with **groups**, **consumption goals**, **contract and commercial policy items**, **campaign items** and **images**.

## When to use this screen

- Query the registered product families.
- Create a new family.
- Link the family to groups, consumption goals, policies and campaigns.

## How to access

Products → Product Family

## Before you start

- Define the family **code**, **description** and **type**.
- Confirm that your profile allows maintaining this registration.

## What the screen does not do

- It does not register the products — only the family and its links.
- It does not control stock.
