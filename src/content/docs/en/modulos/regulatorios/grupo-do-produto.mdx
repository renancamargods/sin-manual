## Overview

The **Product Group** screen (in the Regulatory module) keeps the **regulatory groups** used to classify products from a regulatory point of view. Each group is identified by its **description**.

<Callout type="info" title="Do not confuse with the Product Group (Products module)">
There is also a **Product Group** in the **Products** module (commercial classification). This **Product Group** is in the **Regulatory** module and has a regulatory purpose.
</Callout>

## When to use this screen

- Query the registered regulatory groups.
- Add a new group.
- Fix a group's description.

## How to access

Regulatory → Product Group

## Before you start

- Have the group **description**.
- Confirm that your profile allows maintaining this registration.
