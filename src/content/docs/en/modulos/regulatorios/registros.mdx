## Overview

The **Records** screen keeps the products' **regulatory records** by **country** and **regulatory agency**. Each record stores the **registration number**, the **risk class**, the **listing number** and the **device ID**, serving as the basis for the products' regulatory control.

## When to use this screen

- Register a product's regulatory record in a country/agency.
- Query the records by risk class, number or device ID.
- Fix a record's data.

## How to access

Regulatory → Records

## Before you start

- Have the **country**, the **regulatory agency** and the **registration number**.
- Know the **risk class** and the identifiers (listing number, device ID).
- Confirm that your profile allows maintaining this registration.

## What the screen does not do

- It does not issue the registration at the agency — it only stores the record data.
- It does not register the base product.
