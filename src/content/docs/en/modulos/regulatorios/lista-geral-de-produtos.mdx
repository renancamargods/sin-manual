## Overview

The **General Product List** screen is the **consolidated regulatory view** of the products. It gathers, for each product, the regulatory information — **group**, **line**, **connection**, **status**, **Anvisa registration number** and **GTIN** — and allows **exporting** and **importing** this information in bulk.

## When to use this screen

- Query a product's regulatory information.
- Filter the products by registration, country, status or characteristics (sterile, accessory).
- Export the complete list for analysis.
- Update the information in bulk by spreadsheet import.

## How to access

Regulatory → General Product List

## Before you start

- Know the product, registration or filters you want to apply.
- To import, have the **spreadsheet** in the expected format.
- Confirm that your profile allows querying and maintaining this information.

## What the screen does not do

- It does not register the base product — it gathers its regulatory information.
- It does not issue the Anvisa registration — it only records the number and the status.
