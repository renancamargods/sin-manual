## Overview

The **Regulatory** module brings together the records and settings required by regulatory agencies, including traceability and rework labels and the CE marking of products.

## When to use this module

- Look up the regulatory status of a product.
- Configure label templates by country or process.
- Track records with regulatory agencies.

## How to access

Side menu → Modules → Regulatory

<Callout type="cuidado" title="Requirements by country">
Some records and labels vary according to the product’s destination country. Confirm the local requirement before using.
</Callout>
