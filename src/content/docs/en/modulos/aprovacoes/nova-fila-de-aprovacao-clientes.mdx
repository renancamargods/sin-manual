## Overview

The **New Approval Queue - Customers** screen is where the responsible groups **decide on the customer registrations** that were sent for approval. In it, each customer awaiting release appears in a list, with its status, the cycle progress and the actions to **approve**, **reject** or **send to evaluation**.

It is the queue that receives the customers sent from the **Customer** screen. While the Customer screen creates and forwards the registration, this is where the approval levels record the decision that moves the customer forward (or back) in the approval cycle.

The screen organizes the work into two tabs: **"Customers to Release"** (awaiting the decision) and **"Customers under Evaluation"** (sent for evaluation). See also the [Customer Creation and Approval Flow](/en/tecnico/fluxos/criacao-de-cliente).

## When to use this screen

- Look up the customers awaiting your approval.
- Approve a customer so it moves forward in the approval cycle.
- Reject a registration that does not meet the requirements.
- Send a customer to evaluation when an analysis is needed before deciding.
- Track the number of customers in the queue and the progress of each one.

## How to access

Approvals → New Approval Queue - Customers

## Before you start

- Confirm that your group is responsible for the cycle stage of these customers.
- Have the analysis criteria at hand (registration, credit, tax, as per the level).
- If you are going to reject, know the reason — it guides the consultant to fix the registration.

## Main flows

1. **Find the customer** — enter the filters (Customer, Person Type, Companies) and click **"Refresh"**.
2. **Choose the tab** — use **"Customers to Release"** for those awaiting a decision or **"Customers under Evaluation"** for those sent for evaluation.
3. **Review the registration** — use the row's edit/view icon to check the data before deciding.
4. **Decide** — in the customer's row, use **Approve**, **Reject** or **Send to evaluation**, as per the analysis.
5. **Track** — the totals and the status of each row show the progress; once you decide, the customer moves forward in the cycle.

## What this screen does not do

- Does not create or send customers — that is done on the Customer screen.
- Does not generate the SAP code — it is created when the customer is activated after approval.
- Does not change the approval rules nor define who the approval levels are.
