## Overview

The **Sub-Status - Approval Cycle** screen is where the **sub-statuses** applied in the approval cycle decisions are registered. Each sub-status is linked to an **approval entity** (e.g., "Customer" or "Order"), to a **group** (the responsible level) and to an **application**, which defines when it is used: **on approval**, **on rejection** or **on send to evaluation**.

These are the sub-statuses that appear, for example, in the "Approval Cycle Sub-Status" column of the decision and tracking screens (e.g., "Sales ADM Approved", "Sales ADM Rejected"). When recording the decision of a stage, the system applies the sub-status matching the group and the application.

## When to use this screen

- Register a new sub-status for a group and an approval entity.
- Look up the sub-statuses already registered.
- Change the name, description, application or status of a sub-status.
- Deactivate a sub-status that should no longer be applied.
- Look up the change log of a sub-status.

## How to access

Approvals → Sub-Status - Approval Cycle

## Before you start

- Define the approval entity (e.g., "Customer", "Order") and the group (level) of the sub-status.
- Choose the application: "On Approval", "On Rejection" or "On Send to Evaluation".
- Have a clear name for the sub-status, as it appears in the decision and tracking screens.

## Main flows

1. **Search sub-statuses** — enter the desired filters and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register a sub-status** — click **"+ New"**, enter the "Sub-Status Name", the "Approval Entity", the "Group", the "Sub-Status Application", the "System Status" and the "Description", and click **"Save"**.
3. **Edit a sub-status** — use the row's **Edit** icon, adjust the fields and click **"Save"**.
4. **Look up details** — use the **Details** icon to see the sub-status information.
5. **Look up the log** — use the **Change log** icon to see who changed the sub-status and when.
6. **Deactivate or delete** — set the "System Status" to "Inactive" in the edit window, or use the row's **Delete** icon (red X).

## What this screen does not do

- Does not approve or reject records — it only defines the sub-statuses used in those decisions.
- Does not create the approval entities or the groups — they are registered on their own screens.
- Does not change the approval cycles in progress.
