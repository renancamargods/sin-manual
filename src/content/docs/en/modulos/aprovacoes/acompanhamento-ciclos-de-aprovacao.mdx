## Overview

The **Approval Cycles Tracking** screen lets you **look up and track** the approval cycles opened in the system — for example, the approval of a **Customer** or an **Order**. For each cycle, you can see the overall status, the related record and, in the details, all the stages gone through with the responsible group, the decision and the date.

It is a **tracking-only** screen: the approval or rejection of each stage is recorded by the responsible groups in the approval queues, not here.

## When to use this screen

- Track the progress of a customer or order approval cycle.
- Check which stage the cycle is in and which group is responsible.
- Look up the decision (approved or rejected), the user and the date of each stage.
- Find cycles by status, period or requesting user.

## How to access

Approvals → Approval Cycles Tracking

## Before you start

- Decide how you want to find the cycle (by status, period, entity or requesting user).
- Have at hand, if you know it, the cycle code or the related record (customer or order).
- Confirm that you have permission to access the screen.

## Main flows

1. **Search cycles** — enter the desired filters and click **"Search"**.
2. **Filter by status** — use the **Cycle Status** options (In Progress, Completed, Cancelled) to narrow the list.
3. **Open the details** — click the **Details** icon on the row to see the cycle Information and the Approval Cycle Stages.
4. **Track the stages** — in the details window, see the status, the responsible group, the user and the date of each stage.
5. **Clear filters** — click **"Clear Filters"** to start the search over.

## What the screen does not do

- It does not create approval cycles — they arise from the records (customer, order) according to the rules.
- It does not approve or reject stages — the decision is recorded by the responsible groups in the approval queues.
- It does not change the related record (customer or order).
