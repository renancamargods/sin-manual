## Overview

The **Quote Item** screen registers the items that make up a quote, with the **Item Type**, the **Requested Quantity**, the **Table Price**, the **Unit Price** and the **% Discount**. These items are usually added from within the **Quote** itself, on the items tab.

<Callout type="info" title="Quote item">
The quote items are usually managed within the Quote screen (the "Quote Items" tab). This screen centralizes the registration/lookup of quote items.
</Callout>

## When to use this screen

- Look up quote items.
- Adjust an item's quantity, price or discount.

## How to access

Orders → Quote Item

## Before you start

- Have the **product/item type**, the **quantity** and the **prices**.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the filters and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register/edit** — through the Quote screen ("Quote Items" tab) or through this screen, enter **Item Type**, **Quantity**, **prices** and **discount** and click **"Save"** (or **"Save and Continue"**).
3. **Delete** — click the **Delete** icon on the row.
