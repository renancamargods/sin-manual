## Overview

The **Order Delivery Forecast** screen defines the lead times used to estimate the order delivery: the **Days to Invoice** and the **Days to Deliver**, by **Lounge** and by scope **Type** (Street, City, State or Customer).

## When to use this screen

- Register the invoicing and delivery lead times of a Lounge.
- Define different lead times by scope (street, city, state or customer).
- Look up, edit or delete lead times.

## How to access

Orders → Order Delivery Forecast

## Before you start

- Know the **Lounge** and the scope **Type**.
- Have the **Days to Invoice** and the **Days to Deliver**.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the **"Lounge"** and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register** — click **"+ New"**, select the **Lounge**, enter the **Days to Invoice** and the **Days to Deliver**, choose the **Type** and click **"Save"** (or **"Save and Continue"**).
3. **Edit** — click the **Edit** icon on the row, change it and click **"Save"**.
4. **Delete** — click the **Delete** icon on the row.

## What this screen does not do

- It does not perform the delivery — it only defines the lead times used in the forecast.
