## Overview

The **Order Reason** screen registers the reasons used in the system's orders. Each reason holds an **ERP Identifier Code**, a **Description**, the indication of which **TDC Operation Type** it applies to, the **System Status** and a **Details** field.

## When to use this screen

- Register a new order reason.
- Look up or find a reason.
- Activate or deactivate a reason.

## How to access

Orders → Order Reason

## Before you start

- Have the **ERP Identifier Code** and the **Description**.
- Know the applicable **TDC Operation Type**.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the filters and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register** — click **"+ New"**, enter **ERP Identifier Code**, **Description**, the **TDC Operation Type** and the **Status** and click **"Save"**.
3. **Edit** — click the **Edit** icon on the row, change it and click **"Save"**.
4. **Delete** — click the **Delete** icon on the row.

- It does not create the order — it only registers the reasons used by it.
