## Overview

The **Invoice** screen lets you look up the issued invoices. You can filter by **Invoice Number**, **Series**, **Order Number**, **Customer**, **Lounge** and **issue period**, and view data such as ERP code, payment term, invoice, currency and value.

<Callout type="info" title="Lookup screen">
This is a lookup screen. Invoice issuing happens in other processes; here you find and view the issued invoices.
</Callout>

## When to use this screen

- Find an invoice by number, series, order or customer.
- Look up the invoices issued in a period by Lounge.

## How to access

Orders → Invoice

## Before you start

- Have, if needed, the **invoice number/series**, the **order**, the **customer**, the **Lounge** or the **issue period** to filter.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the filters (number, series, order, customer, Lounge, period) and click **"Search"**.
2. **Analyze** — check the data of the listed invoices.
