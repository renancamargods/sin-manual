## Overview

The **Business Unit** screen registers the business units associated with orders. It is a simple register, identified only by the **Business Unit Description**.

  cards={[

  ]}
/>

## When to use this screen

- Register a new business unit.
- Look up or find a unit by its description.
- Edit or delete a unit.

## How to access

Orders → Business Unit

## Before you start

- Have the **Business Unit Description**.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the **"Business Unit Description"** and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register** — click **"+ New"**, enter the **Description** and click **"Save"** (or **"Save and Continue"**).
3. **Edit** — click the **Edit** icon on the row, change it and click **"Save"**.
4. **Delete** — click the **Delete** icon on the row.
