## Overview

The **Price List** screen registers the commercial price lists used in quotes and orders. Each list has a **Description**, a **Validity** period (Start and End), a **Currency** and a **System Status**, and gathers, on tabs, the linked items, orders and customers.

## When to use this screen

- Register a new price list.
- Define the validity and the currency of the list.
- Review the list's items, orders and customers.

## How to access

Orders → Price List

## Before you start

- Have the **description**, the **validity** period and the **currency**.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the **"Description"** and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register** — click **"+ New"**, enter **Description**, the **validity**, the **Currency** and the **Status** and click **"Save"** (or **"Save and Continue"**).
3. **Edit** — click the **Edit** icon on the row, change it and click **"Save"**.
4. **Review items** — open the list and access the **"Items"** tab.
