## Overview

The **Currency Rate** screen registers the rates used to convert between currencies. Each record has a **Reference Date**, two **Conversion Rates** (Conversion Rate 1 and Conversion Rate 2) and the corresponding **Currency**.

/>

## When to use this screen

- Register a new rate for a currency on a date.
- Look up the rates by reference date.
- Edit or delete a rate.

## How to access

Orders → Currency Rate

## Before you start

- Have the **Reference Date**, the **conversion rates** and the **currency**.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the **"Reference Date"** and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register** — click **"+ New"**, enter the **Reference Date**, the **rates** and the **Currency** and click **"Save"**.
3. **Edit** — click the **Edit** icon on the row, change it and click **"Save"**.
4. **Delete** — click the **Delete** icon on the row.
