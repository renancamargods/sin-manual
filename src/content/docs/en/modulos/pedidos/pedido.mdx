## Overview

The **Order** screen is used to create and track sales orders, from the moment items are added until approval, invoicing and issuance of the invoice. It is one of the most used screens in day-to-day commercial work.

## When to use this screen

- Create a new sales order.
- Add, change or remove items from an order.
- Track the progress and status of an order.
- Look up orders that have already been invoiced.

## How to access

Orders → Order

## Before you start

- Confirm the customer is already registered and active.
- Check the applicable price list and payment terms.
- Confirm whether the order requires approval, according to the configured rules.

## Main flows

1. **Create order** — select the customer, add the items and set the payment term and freight.
2. **Send for approval** — when required, the order goes to the approval queue.
3. **Track invoicing** — once approved, the order moves to invoicing and issuance of the invoice.
4. **Check history** — use the search to find orders by customer, period or status.

## Relationships with other records or processes

The order depends on the customer and product records, and may require approval according to the rules configured in the Approvals module. After invoicing, the order generates the corresponding invoice.

## Important parameters and behaviors

- The order only moves to invoicing after completing the required approvals.
- Changes to items after approval may require a new review, according to the configured rule.
- Some fields may vary depending on the selected business unit.

## Rules and cautions

- Review customer, items and terms before sending the order for approval.
- Avoid changing items that have already been invoiced; use the exchange and return routines when needed.
- Check the applicable freight rule before confirming the order.
- Check your permissions before trying to approve or cancel an order.
