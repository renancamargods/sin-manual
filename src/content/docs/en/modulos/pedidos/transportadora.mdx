## Overview

The **Carrier** screen registers the carriers used to deliver orders. Each carrier holds contact data (Name, Tax ID, Area Code, Phone/Mobile, Email, Contact Name), the **SAP Code**, the **Domestic or International?** indication and the availability options (**Available in Order?**, **Available in Loading List?**), plus the **Address** (its own tab).

## When to use this screen

- Register a new carrier.
- Define the carrier's availability in orders and the loading list.
- Look up, edit or delete carriers.

## How to access

Orders → Carrier

## Before you start

- Have the **contact** data and the **tax ID**.
- Know the **SAP Code** and whether it is **Domestic/International**.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the **"Name"** and/or the **Active** filter and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register** — click **"+ New"**, fill in the contact data, set **SAP Code**, **Domestic/International** and the availability options, enter the **Address** and click **"Save"** (or **"Save and Continue"**).
3. **Edit** — click the **Edit** icon on the row, change it and click **"Save"**.
4. **Delete** — click the **Delete** icon on the row.

## What this screen does not do

- It does not calculate freight — it only registers the carrier (the calculation is in Freight Rule).
