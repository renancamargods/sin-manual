## Overview

The **Currency** screen registers the currencies used in orders, quotes and price lists (for example, BRL, USD, EUR). Each currency holds **Name**, **Symbol**, **Currency Type**, **Person Type** and **Company**, and gathers, on tabs, the related price lists, rates and customers.

## When to use this screen

- Register a new currency.
- Look up or find a currency by name.
- Review a currency's price lists, rates and customers.

## How to access

Orders → Currency

## Before you start

- Have the currency's **name** and **symbol**.
- Know the **currency type**, the **person type** and the **company**.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the **"Name"** and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register** — click **"+ New"**, enter **Name**, **Symbol**, **Currency Type**, **Person Type** and **Company** and click **"Save"** (or **"Save and Continue"**).
3. **Edit** — click the **Edit** icon on the row, change it and click **"Save"**.
4. **Delete** — click the **Delete** icon on the row.

## What this screen does not do

- It does not convert values — it only registers the currency; the rates are in Currency Rate.
