## Overview

The **Journey** screen gathers the registration of the **working journeys** considered in the Field Routing schedule. Each journey defines a **day of the week** and the **start and end times**, delimiting the availability for services.

  cards={[
    { label: 'Purpose', title: 'Working journeys', text: 'Defines the working days and hours.' },
    { label: 'Use', title: 'Basis of the schedule', text: 'The journeys guide the availability in the schedule.' },
    { label: 'Caution', title: 'Day and time', text: 'Check the day of the week and the start and end times.' },
  ]}
/>

## When to use this screen

- Register a journey (working day and time).
- Check the journeys already registered.
- Adjust the day or the times of a journey.

## How to access

Field Routing → Journey

- **Schedule** — the journeys define the days and hours considered in the service schedule.
- **Absence** — the absences reduce the availability of the journeys.
