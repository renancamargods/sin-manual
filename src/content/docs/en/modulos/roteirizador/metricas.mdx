## Overview

The **Metrics** screen gathers the registration of the Field Routing **tracking metrics**. Each metric has a **code**, a **description**, a **weight** and the **mandatory** indication, and serves as an indicator in tracking the services.

  cards={[

  ]}
/>

## When to use this screen

- Register a new metric.
- Check the metrics already registered.
- Adjust the weight or the mandatory flag of a metric.

## How to access

Field Routing → Metrics
