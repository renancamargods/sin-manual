## Overview

The **Field Routing** module organizes schedules, forms and journeys used in field service, allowing you to plan visits and record collected information.

## When to use this module

- Register or look up a schedule.
- Configure forms and their fields.
- Track field service metrics.

## How to access

Side menu → Modules → Field Routing

<Callout type="info" title="Field use">
The information in this module is usually used by teams that carry out in-person visits.
</Callout>
