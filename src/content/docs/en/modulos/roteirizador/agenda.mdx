## Overview

The **Schedule** screen (shown as "Service Schedule") presents, in a **calendar** format, the **appointments** of field visits and services of Field Routing. It is where you **track the service schedule**, according to the configured journeys and schedule types.

<QuickCards
  cards={[
    { label: 'Purpose', title: 'Service schedule', text: 'Shows the appointments of field visits and services.' },
    { label: 'Use', title: 'Tracking', text: 'Displays the schedule in a calendar to track the services.' },
    { label: 'Caution', title: 'Depends on data', text: 'The schedule depends on the appointments, the journeys and the schedule types.' },
  ]}
/>

## When to use this screen

- Check the field service appointments.
- Track the visit schedule according to the journeys.

## How to access

Field Routing → Schedule

<Callout type="info" title="Calendar view">
Unlike the registration screens, the Schedule is shown in a service **calendar**. When opening, the system fetches the appointment information; in environments without appointments, the schedule may appear empty.
</Callout>

## What this screen does not do

- It does not register journeys or schedule types — that is done on the specific screens.
