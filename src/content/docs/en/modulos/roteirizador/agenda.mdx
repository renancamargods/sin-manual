## Overview

The **Schedule** (Appointments Schedule) screen is a **calendar** that shows the scheduled **field appointments**. Appointments appear in their time slots and the view can be by **Day**, **Week** or **Month**, with backward/forward navigation and a **Today** shortcut.

## When to use this screen

- See the scheduled field appointments.
- Navigate the schedule by day, week or month.
- Quickly locate the appointments of a period.

## How to access

Field Routing → Schedule

## Relation to other processes

- **Field Routing (GO.ON)** — the appointments schedule is part of the field service flow. See the [GO.ON - Field Scheduling and Routing](/en/tecnico/fluxos/goon-agenda-e-roteirizacao) flow.
- **Form** and **Journey** — appointments use the forms and journey configured in Field Routing.

## What the screen does not do

- It does not perform the field service itself — that is done in the Field Routing app/execution.
