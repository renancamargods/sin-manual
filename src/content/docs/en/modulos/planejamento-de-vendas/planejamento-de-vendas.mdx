## Overview

The **Sales Planning** screen organizes the planning by **commercial structure**. Access to the planning is done through a cascading selection: first the **GNV** (sales force) and then the **GR or Coordinator**. Based on these choices, the corresponding planning is shown.

## When to use this screen

- Access the sales planning of a sales force (GNV).
- Look up the planning by GR or Coordinator.

## How to access

Sales Planning → Sales Planning

## Before you start

- Know the **GNV** (sales force) to plan (e.g., FVE or FVI).
- Know the corresponding **GR or Coordinator**.

## Main flows

1. **Select the GNV** — choose the sales force in "Select the GNV".
2. **Select the GR or Coordinator** — choose the responsible in "Select the GR or Coordinator".
3. **Access the planning** — the corresponding planning is shown.
