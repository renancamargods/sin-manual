## Overview

The **Quality Analysis** screen drives the **product analysis process** in quality control. From a material document, the products are **scanned**, **classified** as "Vendável" (sellable) or "Sucata" (scrap) and **transferred**, with the result tracked by status (Product, SAP and Deluge).

## When to use this screen

- Look up the quality analyses already registered.
- Scan and classify the products of an analysis.
- Track the transfer status of the processed products.

## How to access

Quality → Quality Analysis

## Before you start

- Have the **material document** and the products to analyze.
- Gather the products with **DataMatrix** for scanning.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the filters and click **"Search"**.
2. **Open the analysis** — use the row's **Details** (or **Edit**) icon.
3. **Classify and scan** — in "Product Entry", choose "Sellable" or "Scrap" and inform the products by Scanning, Copy and Paste or file.
4. **Track** — see the items in "Processed Products" with the Product, SAP and Deluge statuses.
5. **Reprocess** — use **"Reprocessar Produtos"** (Reprocess Products) when needed; **"Save"** keeps the progress.

<Callout type="cuidado" title="Check before transferring">
Transferring the products produces effects (SAP/Deluge integration). Check the items and the classification first.
</Callout>

## What this screen does not do

- Does not register the products — they come from the Product registration.
- Does not register standalone scrap — that is done on the Scrap screen.
