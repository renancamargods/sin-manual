## Overview

The **Quality** module brings together the recording and tracking of the quality analyses carried out on products.

## When to use this module

- Register a new quality analysis.
- Look up the result of analyses already carried out.

## How to access

Side menu → Modules → Quality

<Callout type="info" title="Relationship with Products">
The analyses recorded here are linked to the corresponding product record.
</Callout>
