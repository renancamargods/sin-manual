## Overview

The **Scrap** screen registers and looks up **scrap** — products separated in quality control. Each scrap brings the **lot**, the **product**, the **status** and a **message**.

/>

## When to use this screen

- Register a new scrap.
- Look up the scrap already registered.
- Change the data of a scrap.

## How to access

Quality → Scrap

## Before you start

- Have the **lot** and the **product** of the scrapped item.
- Know the **status** to apply to the scrap.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the filters and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register** — click **"+ New"**, fill in the fields and click **"Save"**.
3. **Edit** — use the row's **Edit** icon, adjust the fields and click **"Save"**.
4. **Look up details** — use the **Details** icon to see the information.

## What this screen does not do

- Does not perform the quality analysis — that is done on the Quality Analysis screen.
- Does not register the products — they come from the Product registration.
