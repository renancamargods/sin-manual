## Overview

The **Find Shipment** screen is a **query** tool in the Invoicing module, used to **locate shipments** based on their identifiers. It is the starting point to find a specific shipment and move on to the other dispatch and invoicing routines.

## When to use this screen

- Locate a specific invoicing shipment.
- Query shipments based on their identifiers.
- Find the shipment as a starting point for other module routines.

## How to access

Invoicing → Find Shipment

## Before you start

- Have at hand the data that identifies the shipment you want to locate.
- Confirm that your profile has access to the screen.

## What the screen does not do

- It does not invoice orders or generate shipments — it only locates them.
- It does not change the data of the found shipments.
