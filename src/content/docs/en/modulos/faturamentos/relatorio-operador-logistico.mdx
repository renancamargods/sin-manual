## Overview

The **Logistics Operator Report** shows the **movements performed by the logistics operator (OPL)**: by customer, order, shipment, product, lot and serial, bringing the **invoices**, **billings**, **values** and **dates** of the OPL stages. The query starts from the center, the order, the invoice or the period.

It is a **query** screen: it generates the report from the filters, without changing data.

## When to use this screen

- Track the movements performed by the logistics operator (OPL).
- Check the invoices and billings of the OPL stages of an order/shipment.
- Locate product, lot and serial in an OPL movement.
- Generate the report for analysis by period or invoice.

## How to access

Invoicing → Logistics Operator Report

## Before you start

- Know the center, SAP order, invoice or period you want to query.
- Confirm that your profile has access to the screen.

## What the screen does not do

- It does not invoice or create movements — it only shows them.
- It does not change invoices, billings or shipments.
