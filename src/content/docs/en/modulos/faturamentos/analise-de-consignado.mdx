## Overview

The **Consignment Analysis** screen **reads directly from SAP** what was **consigned, billed and returned** to the customer (with the invoices) and **compares it with the current consignment balance (MSKU)**. **SAP is the reference** — it is used to check the customer's real consignment position.

## When to use this screen

- Check a customer's consignment balance against SAP.
- Understand what was consigned, billed and returned, with the invoices.
- Investigate a consignment balance divergence.

## How to access

Billing → Consignment Analysis

<Callout type="info" title="What the analysis shows">
From SAP, the query brings: what was **consigned**, what was **billed**, what was **returned** (with the **invoices**) and the **current consignment balance (MSKU)** — for comparison.

## Main flows

1. **Query** — enter the **"Customer code (SAP)"** and click **"Query in SAP"**.
2. **Analyze** — check the consigned/billed/returned and the MSKU balance returned by SAP.

- It does not change the consignment — it only **reads and compares** the position in SAP.
