## Overview

The **Shipments Report** screen queries the **billing shipments** by **order**, **shipment number**, **customer**, **lounge**, **creation period** and **status**. It is used to track the status of shipments (started, completed, cancelled or deleted).

  cards={[

    { label: 'Caution', title: 'Status', text: 'The status shows whether the shipment was completed, cancelled or deleted.' },
  ]}
/>

## When to use this screen

- Track the shipments of an order or customer.
- Find shipments by status (e.g., only **completed** or **cancelled**).
- Query shipments created within a period.

## How to access

Billing → Shipments Report

## Main flows

1. **Query** — enter the filters (order, shipment, customer, lounge, period, status) and click **"Search"**.
2. **Analyze** — see the shipments and their statuses in the **Results**.
3. **Clear** — use **"Clear Filters"** to redo the search.
