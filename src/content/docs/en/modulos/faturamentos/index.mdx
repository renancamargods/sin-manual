## Overview

The **Invoicing** module brings together the routines related to the invoicing process, consignment control and the reports used to track shipments, exchanges and returns.

## When to use this module

- Track the closing of consignment periods.
- Look up shipments awaiting picking.
- Register exchanges and returns.
- Generate consignment, serial and logistics operator reports.

## How to access

Side menu → Modules → Invoicing

<Callout type="info" title="Combined use">
The routines in this module are usually used together with the Orders module, especially at the invoice stage.
</Callout>
