## Overview

The **Serial Status Report** shows the **movement history of a serial**: where the serialized item went through, with the **invoices**, the **operation type**, the lot and the shipment of each movement. The query can start from the **product**, the **serial** or the **invoice**.

It is a **query** screen: it generates the history from the filters, without changing data.

<Callout type="info" title="Screen name">
In the menu, the screen appears as **"Serial Status Report"**; when opened, the header shows **"Serial Movement History"**. It is the same screen.
</Callout>

## When to use this screen

- Track the history of a serialized item.
- See the invoices and operations the serial went through.
- Locate the lot and shipment of a serial.
- Investigate the current situation of a serial.

## How to access

Invoicing → Serial Status Report

## Before you start

- Have the **serial**, the **product code** or the **invoice** for the search.
- Confirm that your profile has access to the screen.

## What the screen does not do

- It does not change invoices, shipments or the product registration.
- It does not move the serial — it only shows its history.
