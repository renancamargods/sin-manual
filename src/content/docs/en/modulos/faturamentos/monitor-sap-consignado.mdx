## Overview

The **SAP Consignment Monitor** screen tracks the **consignment submissions to SAP**: the **status of each attempt**, the **origin** of the submission, the **job** and the **period**. You can filter **only what needs action** and see **only the latest attempt** of each order — it is the screen to find and handle submissions with errors.

## When to use this screen

- Find consignment submissions to SAP with an **error** or **no return**.
- See **only what needs action**.
- Track the **latest attempt** of each order.

## How to access

Billing → SAP Consignment Monitor

<Callout type="atencao" title="Error statuses">
**SAP error**, **Submission error** and **No return from SAP** mean the consignment **was not completed** in SAP and needs **reprocessing/handling**. Use the **"Only what needs action"** filter to focus on these.
</Callout>

## Main flows

1. **Query** — enter the filters (status, origin, period, order/customer) and click **"Search"**.
2. **Focus on what matters** — check **"Only what needs action"** and **"Only the latest attempt"**.
3. **Handle** — analyze the submissions with errors and arrange reprocessing.
