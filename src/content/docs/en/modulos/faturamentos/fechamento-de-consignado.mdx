## Overview

The **Consignment Closing** screen drives the **closing of a consignment**: from a customer and its invoices, the **consignment items** that must be invoiced are gathered, the quantities and amounts are calculated and the **closing** is generated. Each closing starts with a **Protocol** that identifies the operation.

The work is done in two parts: the **listing**, where existing closings are searched, and the **form** ("+ New" or edit), where the items to invoice are selected and the closing is assembled.

## When to use this screen

- Close a customer's consignment.
- Select which consignment items must be invoiced.
- Perform a total or partial closing of the quantities.
- Query and continue closings already started by protocol.

## How to access

Invoicing → Consignment Closing

## Before you start

- Have the **customer** and the reference **invoice** of the consignment.
- Know which **products** and quantities will be invoiced.
- Confirm that your profile allows creating and saving the closing.

## Main flows

1. **Start a closing** — in the listing, click **"+ New"**.
2. **Fetch the information** — in the "Request Header", inform the customer, the invoice and the other data and click **"Fetch Information"**.
3. **Assemble the items** — check the items in "Items to Close" and adjust the **"Qty To Invoice"** (use "Partial" when invoicing only part).
4. **Equalize discount** — if needed, use **"+ Equalize Discount"** to standardize the item discounts.
5. **Save** — review the amounts and click **"Save"** to generate the closing.

<Callout type="cuidado" title="Saving generates the billing">
When saving, the closing generates the billing of the selected consignment items. Review the quantities ("Qty To Invoice"), amounts and discounts before saving.
</Callout>

## What the screen does not do

- It does not register products or customers — they come from their own registrations.
- It does not create the consignment — it only closes it by invoicing the items.
