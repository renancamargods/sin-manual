## Overview

The **Reverse Transfer** screen registers and tracks the **reverse transfers** of products **between centers**. Each transfer receives a **number** and a **status**; the items that come back are **scanned** (by lot and serial) and, at the end, the transfer is **finalized**, with the integration return in **SAP**.

The work is done in two parts: the **listing**, where the transfers are searched, and the **form** ("+ New" or edit), where the operation is assembled and the items are scanned.

## When to use this screen

- Register the reverse transfer of items between two centers.
- Scan the products (lot and serial) that make up the transfer.
- Track the status and the return of the SAP integration.
- Query and continue transfers already started.

## How to access

Invoicing → Reverse Transfer

## Before you start

- Know the **source center** and the **destination center** of the transfer.
- Have the products with **DataMatrix**/serial to scan.
- Confirm that your profile allows creating and finalizing transfers.

## "SAP Return" tab

Gathers the **integration return** of the transfer with SAP, to track the processing in the fiscal/logistics system.

## Main flows

1. **Start the transfer** — click **"+ New"** and inform the "Source Center" and the "Destination Center".
2. **Scan the items** — in "Inform Product", use **"Scanning"** or **"Copy and Paste"** to inform the products that come back.
3. **Check** — track the items in the "Transfer Items" grid and the "Total Items".
4. **Save** — use **"Save"** to keep the progress.
5. **Finalize** — when everything is checked, click **"Finalize Transfer"** to complete and send to SAP.

<Callout type="cuidado" title="Finalizing completes the operation">
"Finalize Transfer" completes the reverse transfer and reflects in SAP. Check the source/destination center and all scanned items before finalizing.
</Callout>

## What the screen does not do

- It does not register products or centers — they come from their own registrations.
- It does not invoice orders — it handles the reverse transfer between centers.
