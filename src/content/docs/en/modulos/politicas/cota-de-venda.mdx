## Overview

The **Sales Quota** screen gathers the registration of the **sales quotas** by **commercial structure** and **period** (month and year). Each quota defines the expected values — **Retail quota**, **KA quota** and **implant quota** (in R$ and in %).

## When to use this screen

- Register the quota of a commercial structure for a period.
- Check the quotas already registered.
- Adjust the Retail, KA or implant quota values.

## How to access

Policies → Sales Quota
