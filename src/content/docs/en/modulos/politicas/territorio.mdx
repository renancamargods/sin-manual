## Overview

The **Territory** screen gathers the registration of the **territories** used in the commercial division. Each territory has a **code**, a **description** and a **country**, and is used in the policies and in the commercial structure.

  cards={[

  ]}
/>

## When to use this screen

- Register a new territory.
- Check the territories already registered.
- Adjust the description or the country of a territory.

## How to access

Policies → Territory
