## Overview

The **Payment Method** screen gathers the registration of the **payment methods** accepted in operations. Each method has a description, a note and the indication of **availability for Comex**, and also gathers, in a tab, the **payment terms** linked to it.

  cards={[

    { label: 'Caution', title: 'Available for Comex?', text: 'Mark the availability for Comex when applicable.' },
  ]}
/>

## When to use this screen

- Register a new payment method.
- Check the methods already registered.
- Check the payment terms linked to a method.

## How to access

Policies → Payment Method
