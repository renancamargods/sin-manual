## Overview

The **Policies** module brings together the commercial rules applied to customers and orders: payment terms and methods, territories, sales quotas and campaigns.

## When to use this module

- Configure a new commercial policy.
- Look up available payment terms and methods.
- Define territories and sales quotas.

## How to access

Side menu → Modules → Policies

<Callout type="cuidado" title="Impact on orders">
Changes to commercial policies apply to new orders and may not affect orders already created.
</Callout>
