## Overview

The **Commercial Structure** screen gathers the registration of the **commercial hierarchy** used in policies and tracking. Each structure has a **code**, a **hierarchy** (level), the responsible **user**, the **superior** and the related roles (advisor, executives, consultants). The screen also concentrates, in tabs, the **contracts, orders, customers and quotas** linked to the structure.

## When to use this screen

- Register a new commercial structure.
- Check the structures already registered and their responsible people.
- Track the contracts, orders, customers and quotas of a structure.

## How to access

Policies → Commercial Structure

## Relationship with other processes

- **Sales Quota** — the quotas are linked to the commercial structures.
- **Territory** — the structure is associated with a territory.
- **Authenticator (User)** — the person responsible for the structure is a system user.
