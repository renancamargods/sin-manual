## Overview

The **Conditional Reward** screen gathers the registration of the **rewards** linked to conditions/targets. Each reward has an **application method**, a **value range** (minimum and maximum), a **validity** and the list of **participants** (the "Reward Participation" tab).

## When to use this screen

- Register a new conditional reward.
- Check the rewards already registered.
- Manage the participants of a reward.

## How to access

Policies → Conditional Reward
