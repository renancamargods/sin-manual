## Overview

The **Payment Term** screen gathers the registration of the **payment terms** used in operations. Each term defines the **payment method**, the **number of installments**, the **deadlines** (days to the first installment and average term) and the link to the **ERP condition**.

  ]}
/>

## When to use this screen

- Register a new payment term.
- Check the terms already registered.
- Adjust the installments, deadlines or payment method of a term.

## How to access

Policies → Payment Term
