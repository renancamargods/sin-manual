## Overview

The **Commercial Policy** screen gathers the registration of the **commercial rules** applied to orders and customers. Each policy has a **type**, a **validity**, an **accounting method** and a **segmentation** (by customer, network, channel, line of business, country, etc.) that defines to whom the rule applies.

## When to use this screen

- Register a new commercial policy.
- Check the policies already registered.
- Adjust the validity, the type or the segmentation of a policy.

## How to access

Policies → Commercial Policy

## Relationship with other processes

- **Orders** and **Customers** — the policies are applied according to the segmentation.
- **Payment Term** and **Campaign Parameter** — complement the commercial rules in campaigns.

## What this screen does not do

- It does not apply the policy to the order itself — it defines the rule; the application occurs in the sales processes according to the segmentation and validity.
