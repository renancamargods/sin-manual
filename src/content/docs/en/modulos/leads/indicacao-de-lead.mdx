## Overview

The **Lead Referral** screen gathers the recording and tracking of **lead referrals** (commercial opportunities). Each referral relates a **requester structure** and a **recipient structure**, associates a **Sales Pipeline** and stores the lead data, the potential value and a brief commercial questionnaire.

## When to use this screen

- Record a new lead referral.
- Check the referrals already recorded and the status of each one.
- Track the most recent interaction and the SLA of each lead.

## How to access

Leads → Lead Referral

## Before you start

- Know the **structures** of the referral's requester and recipient.
- Define the **Sales Pipeline** and the lead **source**.
- Have the **lead data** (name, phone and, when available, tax ID and e-mail) and the **potential value**.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the desired filters and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register** — click **"+ New"**, fill in the lead and questionnaire information and click **"Save"**.
3. **Edit** — use the **Edit** icon on the row, adjust the fields and click **"Save"**.
4. **View details** — use the **Details** icon.

## Relationship with other processes

- **Sales Pipeline** — defines the stages the lead advances through.
- **Leads Kanban Board** — shows the leads in columns by pipeline stage, for visual tracking.

## What this screen does not do

- It does not close the sale — it records and tracks the referral within the sales funnel.
