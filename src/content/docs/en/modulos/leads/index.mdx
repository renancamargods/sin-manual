## Overview

The **Leads** module supports commercial prospecting, bringing together received referrals and the tracking of opportunities until conversion into a customer.

## When to use this module

- Register a new lead referral.
- Track leads on the Kanban board.
- Look up the sales pipeline stage of an opportunity.

## How to access

Side menu → Modules → Leads

<Callout type="info" title="Commercial flow">
Converted leads usually originate a new record in the Customers module.
</Callout>
