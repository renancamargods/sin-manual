## Overview

The **Country** screen keeps the register of countries used throughout the system — in addresses, in the register of states and cities, in customers and in foreign trade operations. Each country holds information such as **Acronym**, **Continent**, **Group** and the associated **Default currency**.

It is a base register: the countries recorded here become available for selection on other screens (such as State, Address and Destination Port).

## When to use this screen

- Register a new country.
- Look up or find an existing country.
- Edit a country's data (acronym, continent, group, default currency).
- Review the states, images and territories linked to the country.

## How to access

Administrator → Country

## Before you start

- Have the country's **name** and **acronym**.
- Know the **continent** and, if applicable, the **group** and **default currency**.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the **"Country Name"** and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register** — click **"+ New"**, fill in the fields on the "Country" tab and click **"Save"** (or **"Save and Continue"** to keep filling in the other tabs).
3. **Edit** — click the **Edit** icon on the row, change the fields and click **"Save"**.
4. **View details** — click the **Details** icon on the row.
5. **Delete** — click the **Delete** icon on the row.

## What this screen does not do

- It does not register states or cities directly — that is done on their own screens (although the "States" tab shows the linked ones).
- It does not convert currency values — it only indicates the country's default currency.
