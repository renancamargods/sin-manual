## Overview

The **State** screen keeps the register of states (identified by an **Acronym**) and the **Country** each one belongs to. States are used in addresses, in the register of cities, in freight rules and in invoicing bindings by Lounge.

## When to use this screen

- Register a new state.
- Look up or find a state by its acronym.
- Edit a state or its country.
- Review the cities, freight rules and invoicing bindings of the state.

## How to access

Administrator → State

## Before you start

- Have the state's **acronym**.
- Know which **country** it belongs to (register the country first, if needed).

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the **"Acronym"** and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register** — click **"+ New"**, enter the **Acronym** and the **Country** and click **"Save"** (or **"Save and Continue"**).
3. **Edit** — click the **Edit** icon on the row, change the fields and click **"Save"**.
4. **View details** — click the **Details** icon on the row.
5. **Delete** — click the **Delete** icon on the row.

## What this screen does not do

- It does not register cities directly — that is done on the City screen (the "Cities" tab only shows the linked ones).
- It does not define freight amounts — it only relates the existing rules.
