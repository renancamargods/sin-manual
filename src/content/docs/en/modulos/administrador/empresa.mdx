## Overview

The **Company** screen registers the group companies (for example, S.I.N Brasil, S.I.N Singular, Biohorizons Camlog Portugal). Each company holds **Company Code**, **Company Name**, **Trade Name**, the linked **Users** and the **System Status**, plus tabs showing the related Lounges, customers, channels, lines of business, classifications and policies.

## When to use this screen

- Register a new group company.
- Look up or find a company by code, name or trade name.
- Edit a company's data or status.
- Review the linked Lounges, customers, channels and policies.

## How to access

Administrator → Company

## Before you start

- Have the company's **code**, **name** and **trade name**.
- Know the **users** that should be linked.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the **"Company Code"** and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register** — click **"+ New"**, enter **Code**, **Name**, **Trade Name**, select the **Users** and the **Status** and click **"Save"** (or **"Save and Continue"**).
3. **Edit** — click the **Edit** icon on the row, change it and click **"Save"**.
4. **Review tabs** — open the company and navigate the Lounges, customers, channels and policies tabs.

- It does not register Lounges, customers or policies directly — the tabs only show the linked ones.
