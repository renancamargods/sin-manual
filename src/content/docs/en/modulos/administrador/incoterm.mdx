## Overview

The **Incoterm** screen keeps the register of Incoterms (international commercial terms, such as CIF, FOB and EXW) used in foreign trade operations. Each Incoterm holds the **Incoterm Code**, the **Name** and the **Incoterm Description**.

## When to use this screen

- Register a new Incoterm.
- Look up or find an Incoterm by its code.
- Edit an Incoterm's name or description.

## How to access

Administrator → Incoterm

## Before you start

- Have the official Incoterm **code** (e.g., CIF, FOB).
- Have the corresponding **name** and **description**.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the **"Incoterm Code"** and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register** — click **"+ New"**, enter **Code**, **Name** and **Description** and click **"Save"** (or **"Save and Continue"**).
3. **Edit** — click the **Edit** icon on the row, change it and click **"Save"**.
4. **Delete** — click the **Delete** icon on the row.

## What this screen does not do

- It does not calculate freight or insurance costs — it only registers the term (Incoterm).
