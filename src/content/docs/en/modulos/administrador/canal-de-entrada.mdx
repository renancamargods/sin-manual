## Overview

The **Entry Channel** screen keeps the register of channels that identify the **origin** of records in the system (for example, Marketing, Website, Customer Referral, Internal, Passive). Each channel holds the **Name**, the associated **Groups** and the **Status**.

  cards={[

  ]}
/>

## When to use this screen

- Register a new entry channel.
- Look up or find a channel by its name.
- Edit a channel's groups or status.

## How to access

Administrator → Entry Channel

## Before you start

- Have the channel's **name**.
- Know the applicable **groups**, if any.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the **"Name"** and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register** — click **"+ New"**, enter **Name**, select the **Groups** and the **Status** and click **"Save"**.
3. **Edit** — click the **Edit** icon on the row, change it and click **"Save"**.
4. **View details / history** — use the **Details** and **History** icons on the row.
5. **Delete** — click the **Delete** icon on the row.
