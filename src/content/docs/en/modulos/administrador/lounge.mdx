## Overview

The **Lounge** screen registers the operation's units (centers) — the Lounges — gathering identification and tax data (Code, Name, Legal Name, Tax ID, State Registration), the **Company** they belong to and several **parameters** that define how the unit operates (invoicing, order integration, foreign trade, card payment, loading list, inventory, among others).

It is a central screen: from the Lounge, additional tabs show the unit's stock, orders, invoices, addresses, freight rules and bindings.

## When to use this screen

- Register a new Lounge unit.
- Look up or find a Lounge by code.
- Edit the tax data, company or operation parameters.
- Review the Lounge's stock, orders, invoices, addresses, freight rules and bindings.

## How to access

Administrator → Lounge

## Before you start

- Have the tax data (Legal Name, Tax ID, State Registration) and the **ERP Code**.
- Know which **Company** the Lounge belongs to.
- Define the **operation parameters** (invoicing, integration, foreign trade, loading list, etc.).

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter **Code** or **ERP Code** and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register** — click **"+ New"**, fill in the tax data, select the **Company**, set the **operation parameters** and click **"Save"** (or **"Save and Continue"** to move through the other tabs).
3. **Edit** — click the **Edit** icon on the row, change it and click **"Save"**.
4. **Review tabs** — open the Lounge and navigate the stock, orders, invoices, address, freight rules and bindings tabs.

## What this screen does not do

- It does not move stock or issue invoices — it only registers the unit and shows the links.
