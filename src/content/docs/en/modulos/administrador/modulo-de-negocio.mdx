## Overview

The **Business Module** screen registers the modules that classify the system's operation types (for example, Orders, Consignment, TDC Exchanges, TDC Returns). Each module holds a **Module Code**, a **Description**, the **System Status** and the associated **Payment Terms**.

## When to use this screen

- Register a new business module.
- Look up or find a module by its code.
- Edit a module's description, status or payment terms.

## How to access

Administrator → Business Module

## Before you start

- Have the module's **code** and **description**.
- Know the applicable **payment terms**.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the **"Module Code"** and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register** — click **"+ New"**, enter **Code**, **Description**, set the **System Status** and the **Payment Terms** and click **"Save"** (or **"Save and Continue"**).
3. **Edit** — click the **Edit** icon on the row, change it and click **"Save"**.
4. **Delete** — click the **Delete** icon on the row.

## What this screen does not do

- It does not run the operation — it only classifies and configures the business module.
