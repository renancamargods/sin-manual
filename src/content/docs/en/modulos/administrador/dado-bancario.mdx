## Overview

The **Bank Details** screen registers the bank accounts used by the units, both **domestic** and **international**. In addition to branch and account data, the screen covers international operation fields (IBAN, SWIFT, ABA, AC. Number) and the **PIX key**, plus the link with the **Lounges**.

<Callout type="atencao" title="Sensitive data">
Bank details are sensitive company information. Carefully check each field and keep the register up to date — incorrect values affect receipts and payments.
</Callout>

## When to use this screen

- Register new bank details (domestic or international).
- Look up or find an account by code.
- Edit an account's data or status.

## How to access

Administrator → Bank Details

## Before you start

- Have the complete account data (branch, account, check digits).
- For international accounts, have IBAN, SWIFT, ABA and AC. Number.
- Know which **Lounge** the account applies to.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the **"Code"** and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register** — click **"+ New"**, choose the **Locality**, fill in the applicable fields, link the **Lounge**, set the **Status** and click **"Save"**.
3. **Edit** — click the **Edit** icon on the row, change it and click **"Save"**.
4. **Delete** — click the **Delete** icon on the row.
