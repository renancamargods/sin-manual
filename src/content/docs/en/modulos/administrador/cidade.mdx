## Overview

The **City** screen keeps the register of cities, each linked to a **State** and also identified by a **Municipality Code**. Cities are used in addresses, streets, freight rules and invoicing bindings by center.

## When to use this screen

- Register a new city.
- Look up or find a city by its name.
- Edit a city's state or municipality code.
- Review streets, freight rules and invoicing bindings of the city.

## How to access

Administrator → City

## Before you start

- Have the city's **name**.
- Know which **state** it belongs to (register the state first, if needed).
- Have the **municipality code** where applicable.

## Main flows

1. **Search** — enter the **"Name"** and click **"Search"**.
2. **Register** — click **"+ New"**, enter **Name**, **State** and **Municipality Code** and click **"Save"** (or **"Save and Continue"**).
3. **Edit** — click the **Edit** icon on the row, change the fields and click **"Save"**.
4. **View details** — click the **Details** icon on the row.
5. **Delete** — click the **Delete** icon on the row.

## What this screen does not do

- It does not register streets directly — the "Streets" tab only shows the linked ones.
- It does not define freight amounts — it only relates the existing rules.
