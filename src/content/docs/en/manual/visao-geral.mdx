## Overview

The S.I.N Implant System is organized into **modules**. Each module brings together the screens and routines related to a single theme — for example, the Customers module concentrates the records related to customers, and the Orders module concentrates everything related to sales orders.

This organization helps you quickly find where to register, look up or track information, even without knowing the exact screen name.

<Callout type="info" title="Access by profile">
Not all users see all modules. What appears available depends on the access profile configured for your user.
</Callout>

## How to navigate

- Use the **side menu** to open a module and see its screens.
- Use the **search** at the top of the page to find a screen by name.
- Each screen indicates, right at the start, the module it belongs to and whether it requires a specific permission.
