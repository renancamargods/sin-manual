## About the glossary

Here we bring together the terms, acronyms and concepts that appear frequently in the system and in this manual. Use the search at the top of the page to find a specific term.

## Commercial and sales

<DocTable
  headers={['Term', 'Meaning']}
  rows={[
    ['Lead', 'Commercial opportunity (potential customer) recorded to be worked by a responsible executive.'],
    ['Sales Pipeline', 'Sequence of stages (statuses) a lead goes through until it is converted.'],
    ['SLA', 'Expected time for a stage; when exceeded, the item may receive a visual alert.'],
    ['Commercial Structure', 'Commercial hierarchy (consultants, managers and other roles) and each one’s customer portfolio.'],
    ['Commercial Policy', 'Set of rules (discounts, campaigns) applied automatically to orders.'],
    ['Sales Restriction', 'Rule that prevents selling certain products to certain customers or situations.'],
    ['Campaign', 'Commercial action with its own pricing and reward rules applied to order items.'],
    ['KA (Key Account)', 'Strategic account or customer, with specific commercial treatment.'],
    ['FVE, FVI, GR, GD', 'Acronyms for roles and hierarchies in the commercial structure.'],
  ]}
/>

## Orders, payment and credit

<DocTable
  headers={['Term', 'Meaning']}
  rows={[
    ['Quick Entry', 'A way to add several items to an order at once.'],
    ['Smart Operation', 'Automatic rules applied when creating certain orders.'],
    ['Mixed Payment', 'Combining more than one payment method in the same order (e.g., PIX + card).'],
    ['Payment link', 'PIX or card link sent to the customer by WhatsApp and email to pay the order.'],
    ['Credit Engine', 'Automatic order review that may query Serasa and decide release, rejection or sending to the credit desk.'],
    ['Approval level', 'Approval level (e.g., Administrative, Credit) the order may need to pass through.'],
    ['Approval Queue', 'List of orders awaiting approval at the levels.'],
    ['Proforma / Invoice', 'Documents used in international orders (COMEX).'],
    ['COMEX', 'Foreign trade; international orders and customers.'],
    ['Drawer Turn (Virada de Gaveta)', 'Internal process of making a lounge’s items available.'],
  ]}
/>

## Logistics, stock and quality

<DocTable
  headers={['Term', 'Meaning']}
  rows={[
    ['DataMatrix', '2D barcode scanned to identify items in picking, inventory, exchanges and quality.'],
    ['GTIN', 'Global product identification barcode (usually 13 digits).'],
    ['Picking Sheet', 'Document generated to guide the physical picking of a shipment’s items.'],
    ['Shipment', 'Set of items of an order prepared for picking and invoicing.'],
    ['Picking', 'Stage of checking the items (by scanning) before invoicing.'],
    ['Consignment', 'Products sent to the customer that are only invoiced when used.'],
    ['Consignment Closing', 'Process of calculating and invoicing the consignment items actually used.'],
    ['Protocol', 'Record that groups the items scanned in exchanges/returns or in consignment closing.'],
    ['Exchanges and Returns', 'Return of items: a return generates credit; an exchange replaces them with other items.'],
    ['InCompany / Intercompany', 'Operation where the invoice is from a center or company different from the user.'],
    ['Loading List (Romaneio)', 'Organizing shipments into a load for transport (carrier, freight and labels).'],
    ['Cyclic inventory', 'Periodic count of only some specific products.'],
    ['Scrap (Refugo)', 'Record of materials rejected or discarded in the quality analysis.'],
  ]}
/>
