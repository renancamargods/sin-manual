## About this manual

This manual brings together, in one place, guidance on how to use the screens and routines of the **S.I.N Implant System**. It was written for the end user: those who register, look up, approve and track information day to day, without needing technical knowledge.

Each module of the system has its own section, with the related screens and routines. Each screen, in turn, always follows the same structure, so you can quickly find what you need: how to access it, what the screen does, which fields to fill in, the main usage flows and the most common questions.

## How the manual is organized

- **SIN System Manual** — introduction, how to access and system overview.
- **Modules** — each system module (Customers, Products, Orders, among others) with its screens and routines.
- **Technical Docs & Settings** — supporting content for configuration and integration teams.

<Callout type="dica" title="How to find what you need">
Use the search at the top of the page to find a screen by name, by module or by a keyword. The side menu also lets you navigate directly through the modules.
</Callout>

## Conventions used in this manual

- **Main fields** — lists the most important fields of each screen and what they are for.
- **Main flows** — step by step of the most common uses of the screen.
- **Rules and cautions** — points of attention before registering, changing or deleting a record.
- **Common problems** — frequent situations and what to check to resolve them.
