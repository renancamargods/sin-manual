## Overview

**Mixed Payment** lets you settle a single order with **more than one payment form** — for example, part in boleto, part in PIX and part in card. Each form becomes a **line** with its **"Amount to Pay"**, and each has its own **status cycle** (token, charge, confirmation), until the order total is paid and it proceeds to the SAP integration.

It complements [Link Generation (PIX, Card and Card Terminal)](/en/tecnico/fluxos/pagamentos-geracao-de-link): the mixed payment reuses the same gateways, but coordinates **several charges** on the same order.

## How to assemble

In the order's **"Mixed Payment Information"**, use **"New Mixed Payment Form"** to add each form. Per line:

- **Payment Form** and **Payment Terms**.
- **Amount to Pay** — how much that form covers.
- For fixed-date boleto, the **Installment Day (Fixed-Date Boleto)**.

The **"Mixed Payment Options"** gather the available forms. A form can be removed (with the confirmation **"Confirm removing the Mixed Payment Form?"**).

<Callout type="atencao" title="Remaining value">
The sum of the mixed payment form amounts **cannot exceed** the order's remaining value. Each line covers a part, and the total must match the order value.
</Callout>

## Relationship with other processes

- **Link generation:** the mixed payment uses the same gateways (PIX via EFI, card via Pagar.me) as [Link Generation](/en/tecnico/fluxos/pagamentos-geracao-de-link).
- **Payment in the order:** form/terms rules in [Order Process — Payment](/en/tecnico/fluxos/processo-do-pedido-pagamento).
- **Value Mapping:** `StatusPagamentoMisto` is in [Database → Value Mapping](/en/tecnico/banco-de-dados/de-para).

## Common problems

- **"Token expired":** generate the charge again — the token that authorizes the payment has a validity.
- **Sum doesn't match:** the sum of the forms cannot exceed the remaining value; adjust the amounts.
- **One form "Payment denied":** that part was refused; the others proceed, but the order only settles when all of them close.
- **Stuck in "Awaiting PIX/card payment":** the charge was generated and awaits the customer's payment.
