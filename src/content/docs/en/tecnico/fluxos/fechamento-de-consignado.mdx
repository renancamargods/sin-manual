## Overview

**Consignment Closing** is the process that **invoices the items left on consignment** at the customer. Instead of invoicing everything up front, the items are shipped on consignment and, as the customer **consumes** (or returns) them, you close the consignment: select what will be invoiced, apply the due discount and **generate the matching order**.

It complements [Order Invoicing and Serial Movement](/en/tecnico/fluxos/faturamento-e-movimento-serial): the closing also produces an invoice and its **serial movement** (source "consignment").

## When to use this flow

- **Close** the items the customer consumed from a consignment.
- Invoice in a consolidated way **by customer** or specifically **by invoice**.
- Record **damage** and apply the **policy discount** at closing.

## Two closing types

## Step 1 — Open the closing

When creating a new closing, the header records:

- **Protocol** — the closing identifier (generated by the system).
- **Closing type** — "By Customer" or "By Invoice".
- **Customer** and/or reference **Invoice**.
- **Damage** — flag for damaged items.
- **Policy discount** — discount applicable per the customer's policy.

## Step 2 — Search and select the items

With the header set, search the available consigned items and select what will be invoiced (**"Select Items by Serial"** option). Each item brings:

  headers={['Field', 'What it is']}

/>

<Callout type="atencao" title="Partial closing">
You don't have to invoice everything at once. The **"Quantity to Invoice"** can be less than the available — the rest stays on consignment for a future closing.
</Callout>

## Step 3 — Equalize the discount

Before generating the order, you can **equalize the discount** of the selected items (closing discount modal). This applies/adjusts the discount uniformly, respecting the policy discount.

## Step 4 — Save and generate the order

1. **"Save"** the closing (the system confirms the closing was saved).
2. **"Generate Order"** — sends the closing to invoicing (marks it **"Sent to invoicing"** with a date) and generates the linked consignment order(s).

The generated orders are listed in the closing (**"Order No."** and **"Qty in Order"**). If that invoice already generated an order, the system warns **"An order was already generated for the selected invoice"**.

## Step 5 — Tracking

- The history is in **"Detailed Status Information"**.
- The **Consignment Report** (Summary/Complete) tracks balances and the consignment note types.
- The generated invoice feeds the **serial movement** (source "consignment") — see the Invoicing flow.

## Relationship with other processes

- **Invoicing and Serial Movement:** the closing generates an invoice and a consignment-source serial movement (`TipoFaturamentoParaMovimentoSerial` = "Consignment via KA Portal" or "Consignment from the CRM") — see [Order Invoicing and Serial Movement](/en/tecnico/fluxos/faturamento-e-movimento-serial).
- **TDC:** a consignment return can also be handled in the [Exchange, Return and Cancellation flow](/en/tecnico/fluxos/troca-devolucao-cancelamento-tdc).
- **Value Mapping:** the codes for `TipoFechamentoConsignado` are in [Database → Value Mapping](/en/tecnico/banco-de-dados/de-para).

## Common problems

- **"An order was already generated for the selected invoice":** that consignment invoice already had a closing; check the order shown.
- **"Quantity to Invoice" rejected:** it cannot exceed the **Quantity Available for Invoicing** of the batch/serial.
- **Item doesn't appear for selection:** confirm the closing type (by customer × by invoice) and whether there is still available balance in that batch/serial.
- **Discount doesn't match:** review the discount equalization and the customer's policy discount.
