## Overview

The **PFCQ Occurrence** registers and drives the analysis of a **product technical complaint / non-conformity** — from the **receipt** of the occurrence to the **technical analysis**, the **procedence** decision and the **closing** (with invoicing when applicable). It is the process that ensures traceability of the complaint, the product and any **regulatory notification** (Anvisa/FDA).

## When to use this flow

- Register a product **technical complaint** or **non-conformity**.
- Drive the **technical analysis** and decide the **procedence**.
- Close the occurrence, with **invoicing** when applicable, and the regulatory **notification**.

## How the occurrence is opened

- **Manually**, registering a new occurrence.
- **From a Survey:** the survey builder has the **"Generate PFCQ Occurrence"** button, which opens the occurrence from the collected answers.

Each occurrence has a **date**, a **customer**, and can record the **SAC number** and the **ticket number**. The occurrence type (`TipoOcorrencia`) can be **PFCQ**, **Lead** or **Service** — this flow handles the **PFCQ**.

## Captured information

The occurrence gathers information in three blocks (`CampoOcorrencia`):

- **Product:** code, batch, UDI and product attachments.
- **Patient:** clinical data of the case (e.g., history, implantation/removal date, bone type, immediate loading, use of SIN instruments and bone graft, intraoral region, quantity).
- **Analysis:** procedence (non-)basis, **FDA** and **Anvisa notification numbers**, **procedence type** and **incidence type**.

## Procedence

In the technical analysis, the complaint's **procedence** is decided (`TipoProcedencia`): **Procedent** or **Not procedent**. The decision supports the **basis** and, when applicable, the **notification** to Anvisa and/or FDA recorded in the occurrence.

## Relationship with other processes

- **Surveys:** a PFCQ occurrence can be born from a survey (the "Generate PFCQ Occurrence" button).
- **Scrap / Quality:** quality analysis and product scrap complement the handling of the non-conformity.
- **Value Mapping:** `StatusOcorrencia`, `TipoOcorrencia`, `TipoProcedencia`, `TipoAtividade` and `CampoOcorrencia` are in [Database → Value Mapping](/en/tecnico/banco-de-dados/de-para).

## Common problems

- **Occurrence stuck in "Verify information":** there is a pending **awaiting documentation/information** activity.
- **Cannot close:** confirm the **procedence** in the technical analysis and the **approval/closing** activities.
- **Missing regulatory notification:** check the **Anvisa/FDA** notification number fields in the analysis.
