## Overview

**Reverse Transfer** moves items **back** from one Lounge to another — from a **source Center** to a **destination Center**. The returning items are **scanned** (DataMatrix reading, with batch and serial), the transfer is saved and, after the **SAP return**, it is finished. It is the step that closes traceability when an item needs to go back to another unit's stock.

It complements the other Invoicing flows ([Serial Movement](/en/tecnico/fluxos/faturamento-e-movimento-serial), [TDC](/en/tecnico/fluxos/troca-devolucao-cancelamento-tdc) and [Consignment Closing](/en/tecnico/fluxos/fechamento-de-consignado)), handling specifically the **physical reverse movement** between Lounges.

## When to use this flow

- Return items from one Lounge (Center) to another.
- Record, with traceability, the items going back to the destination stock.
- Consolidate the return with the **SAP** confirmation.

## Step 1 — Open the transfer

In **"+ New"**, on the **"Transfer Data"** tab, enter:

- **Source Center** — the Lounge the items leave from.
- **Destination Center** — the Lounge that receives the items back.

The system generates the **transfer number** (pattern `TR000001`) and the transfer starts with the status **"Started"**.

## Step 2 — Scan the transfer items

Still on the "Transfer Data" tab, **scan** the returning items (DataMatrix reading). Each scanned item enters the grid with:

<DocTable
  headers={['Field', 'What it is']}
  rows={[
    ['Code', 'Code of the scanned product.'],
    ['Description', 'Product description.'],
    ['Batch', 'Batch read from the item.'],
    ['Serial', 'Serial read from the item.'],
    ['Scan Date/Time', 'When the item was scanned.'],
    ['User', 'Who performed the scan.'],
  ]}
/>

<Callout type="info" title="Lot/serial traceability">
Each item carries the **scanned DataMatrix**, the **batch** and the **serial** — that is what keeps the item's traceability when it returns to the destination Center.
</Callout>

## Step 3 — Save

Use **"Save"** to record the transfer with the scanned items. It stays **"Started"** while awaiting the SAP return.

## Step 4 — SAP return

On the **"SAP Return"** tab, follow the integration return — the confirmation, in SAP, of the reverse movement of the items between the Centers.

## Step 5 — Finish the transfer

With the SAP return confirmed, use **"Finish Transfer"**. The transfer moves to the **"Completed"** status and the items are recorded in the destination Center's stock.

## Relationship with other processes

- **Serial Movement:** the reverse transfer preserves the lot/serial traceability already used in [Order Invoicing and Serial Movement](/en/tecnico/fluxos/faturamento-e-movimento-serial).
- **Value Mapping:** the codes for `StatusTransferenciaReversa` and `TipoDaOperacaoReversa` are in [Database → Value Mapping](/en/tecnico/banco-de-dados/de-para).

## Common problems

- **Transfer stuck in "Started":** confirm the **SAP return** on the matching tab before finishing.
- **Item won't scan:** check the DataMatrix reading (batch/serial) — only items with a valid reading enter the grid.
- **Wrong destination Center:** review the source and destination Centers on the "Transfer Data" tab before saving.
