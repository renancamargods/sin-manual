## Overview

The **Customer Creation and Approval** flow describes the complete process of registering customers in the system, for the types **Individual (PF)**, **Company (PJ)** and **Foreign**. The process goes from pre-registration, through filling in the data, to sending for the approval levels and the automatic integration with SAP when the customer is approved.

The flow happens in the **Customer Module**, mainly on the **Customer** and **Registration Approvals Queue** screens, and involves two types of user: the one who creates the customer (Master or Common) and the one who approves (Credit, Tax and International Credit levels).

## When to use this flow

- Register a new customer (individual, company or foreign).
- Send a newly registered customer for review by the levels.
- Approve, reject or review a customer in the approval queue.
- Track the registration status until activation and SAP code generation.

## How to access

- Customer Module → Customer Screen
- Customer Module → Registration Approvals Queue

## Before you start

- Have the username and password of the desired profile (Master or Common).
- Know the **person type** (Individual, Company or Foreign) and the **registration type** (Student, Guarantor, Foreign, Patient or Retail).
- Have at hand the customer’s document (CPF or CNPJ) and the required data (address, phone, email and credential).

## Step by step of the process

### 1. Login and access to the Customer Module

1. **Login** — access the system and enter the username and password of the desired profile (Master or Common). Click **Sign in**.
2. **New registration** — in the menu, go to **Customer Module → Customer Screen** and click **"+ New Customer"**. The system opens the pre-registration screen.

<Callout type="dica" title="Test with both profiles">
When validating the process, run the tests with both the **Master User** and the **Common User** (e.g., Consultant), to ensure the permissions are correct.
</Callout>

### 2. Pre-registration (Pulse Credit integration)

On the pre-registration screen, fill in:

Click **Next** to proceed to the full registration.

### 3. Full registration — Registration Data tab

Fill in the customer’s required fields:

After filling everything in, click **Save** or **Save and Continue**. The **"Send Customer"** button becomes enabled.

### 4. Send for review

1. Click **"Send Customer"**.
2. In the confirmation popup, click **YES**.

The customer is routed to the approval queue according to the person type:

<DocTable
  headers={['Person type', 'Approval queue(s)', 'Responsible']}

  ]}
/>

### 5. Status tracking

After sending, the customer stays on the customers screen with the status **"Under Review by Registration/Credit"**.

### 6. Approval in the Registration Approvals Queue

1. Log in with the user responsible for the corresponding level.
2. Go to **Customer Module → Registration Approvals Queue** and find the customer.
3. Choose one of the available actions:

- **Approve** — approves the customer registration.
- **Reject** — rejects the customer registration.
- **Review Customer** — detailed review before deciding.
- **Send for Evaluation** — forwards for additional review in the Customers Under Evaluation tab.
- **Add Note** — records a note on the registration.

## Rules and cautions

- All required fields must be filled in before saving; the **"Send Customer"** button is only available after saving successfully.
- The approval level varies according to the person type — check the send-for-review table.
- The SAP code is only generated on approval (ACTIVE status).
- Each profile should have access only to what is needed for its role; level credentials should not be shared.
