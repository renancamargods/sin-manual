## Overview

**Sales Planning** distributes and adjusts the **sales targets/quotas** across the **commercial structure** — from the top (Board/GNV) to the **consultant** — by period (month/year). Each level adjusts its share and the system **records who changed** what, ensuring traceability of the planning.

## When to use this flow

- **Set** the sales quotas of a period (month/year).
- **Distribute** the target across the commercial structure, level by level.
- **Adjust** a level's quota and track the change history.

## Sales quota

The **Sales Quota** is defined by **commercial structure** and by **month/year**, with targets by segment (e.g., **Retail**, **KA** and **Implants**), in **value (R$)** and **percentage (%)**. Each level receives and distributes its quota to the levels below.

<Callout type="info" title="Access flow">
The planning flows down the hierarchy: upper levels (GNV) set and pass down, and the levels below (GR, GD, Coordinator, Consultant) receive and adjust their share.
</Callout>

## Change record

Each change is recorded in a log (`TipoLogPlanejamentoDeVendas`): **changing user**, **change date**, **name** and **profile** of who changed — allowing the planning to be audited.

## Common problems

- **Quota doesn't match the sum:** the distribution per level must close with the target of the level above.
- **Cannot adjust:** the adjustment depends on the level/profile (changer context).
- **Who changed it?:** check the change log (user, date, name and profile).
