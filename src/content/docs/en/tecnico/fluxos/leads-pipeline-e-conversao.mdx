## Overview

The **Leads** flow drives an opportunity from **entry** (referral/qualification) to **closing**, moving the lead through the columns of the **pipeline** on the **Kanban Board**. Each column is a **status** of the pipeline, grouped into two **phases** — **Negotiation** and **Closing** — and the lead advances as interactions happen, with **history** and **SLA alerts** so opportunities don't stall.

## When to use this flow

- **Register and qualify** a new lead.
- **Move** the lead through the pipeline as the negotiation evolves.
- **Close** the lead as won (converts) or lost.

## Step 1 — Lead entry and qualification

When registering the lead (e.g., **Lead Referral**), you set:

- The lead **origin** (`OrigemLead`) — today **Internal**.
- The lead **type** (`TipoLead`) — **no registration** or **SIN base** (existing).
- The **interest level** (`NivelInteresseLead`) — just browsing, interested or very interested.
- The **potential items** — products/items of interest to the lead.

## Step 2 — Pipeline and Kanban

The lead enters the **sales pipeline**. On the **Kanban Board**, each **column** is a **status** of the selected pipeline, shown in the **pipeline order** and grouped by **phase** (`FaseStatusLead`):

<DocTable
  headers={['Value', 'Phase', 'What it gathers']}
  rows={[
    ['0', 'Negotiation', 'The statuses of the negotiation progress (e.g., new, presentation, proposal, analysis).'],
    ['1', 'Closing', 'The outcome statuses — won and lost.'],
  ]}
/>

Each status has a **probability %** and a **pipeline order**. The board **scrolls horizontally** through the columns.

<Callout type="info" title="Configurable columns">
The statuses (columns) are defined in the **Sales Pipeline**. Each pipeline has its own set of statuses; the Kanban shows exactly the statuses of the selected pipeline.
</Callout>

## Step 3 — Move through the funnel

As the negotiation evolves, the lead is **moved** across columns. Each advance/record stays in the lead's **Interaction History**. To avoid stalled opportunities, there are **Status Alerts** with **SLA** — the deadline is measured by an interval (`TipoDeIntervaloSLA`):

<DocTable
  headers={['Value', 'SLA interval']}
  rows={[
    ['0', 'Minutes'],
    ['1', 'Hours'],
    ['2', 'Days'],
    ['3', 'Months'],
  ]}
/>

## Step 4 — Closing (won or lost)

In the **Closing** phase, the lead ends as:

- **Won** — the opportunity converts (proceeds to customer/order).
- **Lost** — the opportunity is closed without conversion.

## Relationship with other processes

- **Customer and Order:** a **won** lead proceeds to the creation/registration of the customer and the order — see [Customer Registration](/en/tecnico/fluxos/criacao-de-cliente) and [Order Process](/en/tecnico/fluxos/processo-do-pedido).
- **Value Mapping:** `FaseStatusLead`, `NivelInteresseLead`, `TipoLead`, `OrigemLead` and `TipoDeIntervaloSLA` are in [Database → Value Mapping](/en/tecnico/banco-de-dados/de-para).

## Common problems

- **Columns different from expected:** the Kanban shows the statuses of the **selected pipeline** — check which pipeline is active.
- **Stalled lead raising an alert:** the status SLA was exceeded; handle the opportunity or move it across columns.
- **Cannot close:** confirm the lead is in the **Closing** phase (won/lost).
