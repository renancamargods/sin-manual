## Overview

The **Customer Registration WITHOUT Ignition** flow describes, step by step, how to register and approve a new customer using the **queue WITHOUT Ignition** — a faster and more direct approval path. It applies to **Individual**, **Company** and **Foreign** customer types.

Whenever a new customer is registered, it goes through an **approval flow** before becoming active. This flow ensures the registration is reviewed and released correctly, and the path changes slightly depending on the customer type. The process has two parts: the **system preparation** (done once by the administrator) and the **customer registration and approval** (day-to-day use).

## When to use this flow

- Register a new customer (individual, company or foreign) through the queue WITHOUT Ignition.
- Prepare the approval rule and steps used by this flow.
- Send a newly registered customer to the approval steps.
- Track the status and the approval cycle sub-status until activation and SAP code generation.

## How to access

- Administrator Module → Feature Control (preparation)
- Approvals Module → Approval Rule (preparation)
- Customers Module → Customer (day-to-day registration)

## Before you start

- Confirm with the administrator that the **approval rule** and the **steps** are already configured.
- Know the **person type** (Individual, Company or Foreign) and the **registration type** of the customer.
- Have the customer **document** (Tax ID, when applicable) — it is required.

## Part 1 — System preparation (done by the administrator)

These steps are normally already configured. They only need to be done once, before starting to register customers.

### Step 1 — Define whether the queue uses Ignition

In the **Administrator** module, **Feature Control** screen, you can enable or disable the use of the Ignition queue. For this process, the option must be set **so that the Ignition queue is NOT used** — that is, the registration follows the simplest and fastest path.

### Step 2 — Configure the Approval Rule

In the **Approvals** module, **Approval Rule** screen, define the **rule name**, the **approval entity** and the **execution mode**:

<DocTable
  headers={['Execution mode', 'What it means']}
  rows={[
    ['Parallel', 'There is no order between the approvers.'],
    ['Sequential', 'Approval follows the registered order.'],
  ]}
/>

### Step 3 — Register the approval steps

Within the created rule, click **"New approval step"** (or edit an existing one). Fill in the **responsible group**, the **order** and the **applicable person types**, keeping the step **ACTIVE** so it works in the process.

## Part 2 — Customer registration and approval (day-to-day use)

With the system prepared, this is the path to follow whenever a new customer needs to be registered.

### Step 1 — Open the new customer registration

Access the **Customers** module, **Customer** screen, and click the **"+ New Customer"** button.

### Step 2 — Select the customer type and enter the document

Choose the **person type** (Individual, Company or Foreign) and the **registration type**. Enter the **document** (Tax ID, when applicable), which is required, and click **"Next"**.

### Step 3 — Fill in the customer data

The system opens the registration screen. If data already exists for that document, the system fills it automatically (the **"verify"** function). Otherwise, fill in all required fields manually.

### Step 4 — Save and send the customer

After filling everything in, click **"Save and continue"**. The **"Send customer"** button appears — click it to send the registration to the approval flow.

### Step 5 — Check the registration status

Look for the customer in the **customer list** to follow the registration. You can filter by **Person Type** (Individual, Company or Foreign) and see the **registration status**, the **most recent update date** and the **approval cycle sub-status**.

Approvals follow exactly what was configured in the **Approval Rule**. Today, the rule is set up as follows:

<Callout type="info" title="The rule drives the flow">
The approval path of each person type comes from the Approval Rule. If the steps or the order change in the rule, the flow changes with them — always check the current configuration.
</Callout>

## Final activation of the registration

After a few seconds or minutes, the system **activates the registration automatically**, regardless of the customer type. At that point you can see the **most recent update** and the **SAP Code**, generated by the system itself to identify that customer. From there, the process is complete and the customer is ready to use.

## Quick summary

1. Prepare the queue without Ignition and configure the approval rule (once).
2. Register the new customer with the required data, entering the correct person type.
3. Send the customer for approval.
4. Track the status in the customer list.
5. Wait for the approvals according to the customer type (Individual, Company or Foreign).
6. Check the final activation and the generated SAP Code.

## Rules and cautions

- The **Ignition** option must be configured so that the Ignition queue is **not** used in this flow.
- Approvals follow the **Approval Rule**: the mode (Parallel/Sequential), the active steps and the applicable person types determine the path.
- The **document** (Tax ID) is required at the start of the registration.
- The **"Send customer"** button appears after **"Save and continue"**.
- The **SAP Code** is only generated when the registration is activated.
