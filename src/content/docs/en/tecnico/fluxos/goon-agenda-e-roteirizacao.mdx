## Overview

The **Router** flow organizes **field services** — pickup, delivery, installation, maintenance, visit and others — and **integrates them to GO.ON**, which generates the **Service Order (OS)** and drives the execution on **mobile**. From the **schedule** (customer, address, date and type), the system controls **availability** (journey and absences), applies a service **form** and tracks **metrics**.

## When to use this flow

- **Schedule** a field service for a customer.
- **Integrate** the service to GO.ON and generate the **OS**.
- **Collect** field information by form and track **metrics**.

## Step 1 — Build the schedule

In the **Schedule**, the service data is recorded:

- **Customer** and **address** (street, number, city, state, ZIP, reference) and **contact** (phone/email).
- **Dates** (window start/end and service date) and whether the time is **flexible**.
- **Schedule Type** (with an indication if it is a **visit**) and **notes**.

## Step 4 — Integrate to GO.ON

The **"Integrate Schedule"** action sends the service to **GO.ON**, which generates the **OS** (the CRM stores the **GO.ON OS number** and marks the schedule as **integrated**). Each service corresponds to a **service type** (`TipoServicoGoon`):

<Callout type="info" title="OS generated by GO.ON">
The schedule can also record that the **OS was generated by GO.ON itself** — in that case the service is born there and reflected in the CRM.
</Callout>

## Step 5 — Field execution and metrics

With the OS generated, the service is executed on **GO.ON Mobile**: the professional fills in the **form** (text/image answers) and the execution feeds the **Metrics** (code, description, **weight** and whether it is **required**), used to assess the service.

## Relationship with other processes

- **Router screens:** Schedule, Form, Form Field, Field Type, Journey, Absence, Metrics and Schedule Type support this flow.
- **Value Mapping:** `TipoServicoGoon`, `TipoAusencia` and `TipoCampo` are in [Database → Value Mapping](/en/tecnico/banco-de-dados/de-para).

## Common problems

- **Schedule doesn't become an OS:** confirm the **"Integrate Schedule"** action — without integrating, it is just a CRM appointment.
- **Time conflict:** check the professional's **journey** and **absences**.
- **Form with no fields:** attach a form with fields (text/image) before the service.
- **Reimbursement doesn't appear to create:** the "Reimbursement" type can only be created from **Mobile**.
