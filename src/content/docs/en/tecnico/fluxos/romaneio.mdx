## Overview

The **Loading List (Romaneio)** flow standardizes the shipment dispatch process of the S.I.N Implant System, from the initial setup of the records to the creation, checking, collection and sending of the shipment. The goal is to ensure standardization across operators, shipment traceability and compliance with the company’s quality criteria.

The flow goes through the screens of the **Orders**, **Administrator** and **Invoicing** modules, and ends with sending the collected shipment to **SimuleFrete**.

## When to use this flow

- Prepare a shipment for dispatch, from picking to collection.
- Generate and print the volume labels of a shipment.
- Group shipments into a loading list and check their volumes.
- Record the collection and send the loading list to SimuleFrete.

## How to access

The screens involved in this flow are:

- Orders → Carrier
- Administrator → Center (Lounge)
- Administrator → Label Printer
- Invoicing → Shipments Awaiting Picking Queue
- Invoicing → Label Generation Queue
- Invoicing → Loading List Queue

## Before you start

Before operating the flow day to day, the records below must be configured.

### Carrier (Orders module)

In **Orders → Carrier**, the **"Available in the Loading List?"** option must be set to **Yes** so that the carrier appears in the selection during the shipment. It is this setting that makes the carrier appear in the selection field, both in the Shipments Awaiting Picking Queue and at the picking finalization.

### Center / Lounge (Administrator module)

In **Administrator → Center (Lounge)**, the **"Available for the Loading List Flow?"** option defines the lounge path:

- **Yes** — the lounge starts to appear in the Shipments Awaiting Picking Queue, entering the loading list flow.
- **No** — the lounge follows the standard operational flow, without going through the loading list.

## Step by step of the process

### 1. Shipments Awaiting Picking Queue

Invoicing module.

1. **Lounge identification** — when the screen opens, the system checks the shipment’s source lounge and applies the settings from the records stage. If the lounge is enabled and the carrier is correctly configured, the **Carrier** field is shown.
2. **Scanning and saving** — the operator scans the shipment products and saves the information.
3. **Picking finalization** — when clicking **"Send Order for Invoicing"**, the **"Finalize Picking"** modal opens with two required fields: **Volumes** (number of labels for the shipment) and **Carrier**.
4. **Confirmation and sending to SAP** — the operator clicks **"Confirm and Send to SAP"** (green button) and the system sends the shipment for invoicing.

<Callout type="info" title="How to fill in the finalization">
The **Carrier** field already comes filled in with the order information and the SAP return; change it only if necessary. The **Volumes** field must be entered by the operator. When finished, the system returns to the initial screen and shows the message **"Reading saved successfully"**.
</Callout>

### 2. Label Generation Queue

Invoicing module.

1. **List of completed shipments** — the screen shows only shipments that are completed and sent to invoicing. By default, the **"With Carrier?"** and **"With Volume?"** filters are set to **Yes**.
2. **Opening the shipment** — the operator selects the shipment and clicks **"Edit"**, opening the "Shipment Label Generation" screen with the shipment, order, customer, carrier and volume data.
3. **Generating and printing the labels** — when clicking **"Generate Labels"**, the system shows the labels and enables the **Print** button (individual or **"Print All"**). When printing, the **"Select Printer"** popup opens with the registered printers.
4. **Sending to the loading list** — after printing all labels, the **"Send to Loading List"** button is enabled. When triggered, the shipment leaves this queue and appears in the Loading List Queue.

<Callout type="atencao" title="Label printing">
When physical label printing is disabled in the system settings, it is possible to move forward in the flow without printing. Otherwise, all labels must be printed to release the sending to the loading list.
</Callout>

### 3. Loading List Queue

Invoicing module. The screen has two tabs: **Shipments Awaiting Loading List** and **Created Loading Lists**.

**"Shipments Awaiting Loading List" tab**

1. **Shipment selection** — select at least one available shipment and click **"Create Loading List"**.
2. **Loading list creation** — the system opens the "Create Loading List" screen with the selected shipments. After confirming, the loading list appears in the **Created Loading Lists** tab with the initial status **Under Checking**.

**"Created Loading Lists" tab — status cycle**

Each loading list goes through four statuses, each on a tab of the detail screen:

1. **Under Checking** — initial status. You can edit or delete the loading list. When clicking **Edit**, the **"Volume Checking"** enables scanning by barcode (pattern **VOL-NNN-SHIPMENT**). After checking all volumes, the **"Generate Document"** button is enabled.
2. **Checked** — when clicking **"Generate Document"**, the loading list moves to the **Document** tab and opens the "Loading List Checking" screen. When clicking **Print**, the loading list PDF is generated.
3. **Collection** — on the **Collection** tab, fill in the required data and click **"Confirm Collection"**. The status changes to **Collected**.
4. **Collected / Sent** — after confirming the collection, the flow sends the loading list to SimuleFrete. Once the sending is complete, the final status becomes **Sent**, ending the shipment cycle.

<Callout type="atencao" title="Send to SimuleFrete button">
While the automatic integration with SimuleFrete is temporarily unavailable, the **"Send to SimuleFrete"** button appears as an alternative. It is shown whenever the loading list is in the **Collected** status and has not been sent yet.
</Callout>

## Rules and cautions

- The Label Generation Queue only shows shipments with **carrier** and **volume** filled in; without this data, the shipment does not appear.
- The **"Generate Document"** button is only enabled after scanning **100% of the volumes** of the shipment.
- Each volume’s barcode must follow the **VOL-NNN-SHIPMENT** pattern; discrepancies prevent the checking.
- The **"Send to SimuleFrete"** button only appears after the shipment reaches the **Collected** status.
- Changes to the carrier or center/lounge settings take immediate effect on the operational screens; validate the impact before changing records in production.
