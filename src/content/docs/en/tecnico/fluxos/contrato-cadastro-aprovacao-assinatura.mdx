## Overview

The **Contract** flow drives the document from **draft** to **approval** by the responsible groups, to **electronic signature** (via **ZapSign**) and, when it is lending (comodato), to the **generation of lending orders** — besides handling **renewal**, **amendment** and **cancellation**. It is the process that formalizes the commercial conditions (discounts, partnership, dental or lending) agreed with the customer.

## When to use this flow

- **Create** a contract (discounts, partnership, dental or lending).
- **Approve** and **sign** the contract electronically.
- **Generate** the lending orders and handle **renewal/amendment/cancellation**.

## Step 1 — Build the contract (draft)

The contract starts as **"Draft"**. In it the conditions are defined — depending on the type — such as **contract policy**, **consumption volumes**, **discounts**, **consumption targets** and **lending items**. The **consumption target** can be applied by (`AplicacaoMetaConsumo`):

## Step 2 — Send for approval

Once the draft is done, the contract goes to **"Awaiting approvals"** and passes through the **responsible groups** (`GrupoResponsavelAprovacaoContrato`):

<DocTable
  headers={['Value', 'Responsible group']}
  rows={[
    ['0', 'ADM'],
    ['1', 'FP&A (financial planning)'],
    ['2', 'Credit'],
    ['3', 'Operations / Customer Service'],
  ]}
/>

If the cycle is interrupted, the contract goes to **"Approval cycle canceled"**.

## Step 3 — Electronic signature (ZapSign)

Once approved, the contract is **integrated to ZapSign** for signature. The status tracks:

- **"ZapSign integrated"** — sent for signature.
- **"Awaiting signature completion"** — signatures in progress.
- **"Signatures completed"** — everyone signed.

## Step 4 — Lending (order generation)

For **lending** contracts, after the signatures the contract goes to **"Awaiting lending order generation"** and then **"Lending order generated"**. Lending items can have the operation (`TipoOperacaoComodato`): **Lending**, **Bonus** or **Consignment**.

## Step 5 — Renewal, amendment and cancellation

- **Renewal:** generates a new contract and deactivates the previous one (**"Deactivated by renewal generation"**).
- **Amendment:** generates an amendment and deactivates the previous one (**"Deactivated by amendment generation"**).
- **Cancellation:** closes the contract (**"Canceled"**).

## Course contract

Contracts can involve **courses** (classes, participants and periods). The course status (`StatusDeCurso`) is **Current** or **Expired**.

## Relationship with other processes

- **Approvals:** the contract uses the queue and rules of [Approvals](/en/modulos/aprovacoes) (responsible groups).
- **Lending orders:** lending contracts generate orders — see [Order Process](/en/tecnico/fluxos/processo-do-pedido).
- **Value Mapping:** `StatusDoContrato`, `SituacaoContrato`, `TipoDoContrato`, `ClassificacaoDoContrato`, `GrupoResponsavelAprovacaoContrato`, `AplicacaoMetaConsumo`, `TipoOperacaoComodato` and `StatusDeCurso` are in [Database → Value Mapping](/en/tecnico/banco-de-dados/de-para).

## Common problems

- **Contract stuck in "Awaiting approvals":** a responsible group is pending approval.
- **Signature doesn't complete:** follow the ZapSign status ("Awaiting signature completion").
- **Lending with no orders:** the contract must have **signatures completed** before generating the lending orders.
- **Renewal/amendment doesn't replace:** when generated, the previous contract is deactivated (status 97/98).
