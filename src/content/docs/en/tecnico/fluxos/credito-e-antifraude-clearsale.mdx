## Overview

During the **customer registration analysis**, the system queries **ClearSale** to support the credit decision and fraud prevention: **fraud score**, **insights**, **ratings**, **background** (individual/company) and, when applicable, **facial biometrics**. The result feeds the Credit area's analysis and the customer's **credit classification**.

This flow complements [Customer Registration × Approvals](/en/tecnico/fluxos/criacao-de-cliente): the ClearSale query happens in the analysis step before the customer is released.

## When to use this flow

- **Analyze the credit** of a customer in registration/re-analysis.
- **Prevent fraud** with ClearSale's score and background.
- Request **facial biometrics** when identity validation is needed.

## What is queried on ClearSale

- **Fraud Score** — fraud risk score.
- **Insights** and **Ratings** of the customer — analysis indicators.
- **Background** (individual/company) — registration and restrictive information (e.g., Sintegra, Simples Nacional, CNAE, addresses, phones).
- **Facial Biometrics** — identity validation by selfie (optional).

## History and traceability

Each query is recorded in the **ClearSale Transaction History** and in the **query / module request logs** — ensuring traceability of the analysis and of what was queried.

## Relationship with other processes

- **Customer Registration × Approvals:** the ClearSale analysis happens within the credit step of the customer approval — see [With Ignition](/en/tecnico/fluxos/criacao-de-cliente) and [Without Ignition](/en/tecnico/fluxos/cadastro-clientes-sem-ignition).
- **Value Mapping:** `StatusBiometricoClearSale`, `TipoSolicitacaoClearSale`, `TipoSolicitacaoBiometricaClearSale`, `TipoDeEnvioBiometriaFacialClearSale` and `ClassificacaoDeCreditoDoCliente` are in [Database → Value Mapping](/en/tecnico/banco-de-dados/de-para).

## Common problems

- **Biometrics "EXPIRED":** the link was not completed in time; resend a new link.
- **Query with no return:** check the transaction history and the ClearSale query logs.
- **Divergent classification:** the credit classification results from the analysis; review the queried services (score/background).
