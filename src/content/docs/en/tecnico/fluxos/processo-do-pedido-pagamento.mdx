## Overview

This is **Part 4** of the [Order Process](/en/tecnico/fluxos/processo-do-pedido). Here we detail the **payment**: the order uses a **Payment Term** (which carries the **Payment Method**). The most common methods are **boleto**, **PIX** and **card**, and you can combine methods in a **mixed payment**.

The chosen method defines its own fields and rules — for example, **boleto** asks for the due day, and **PIX/card** go through a token/link flow with their own statuses. The method also influences the **approval**: orders with **PIX or card** trigger the **Collection** step.

## When to use

- Choose the order's payment method and term.
- Split the payment across more than one method (mixed payment).
- Understand the payment statuses (token, PIX, card) and why collection came in.

## Mixed payment

The **mixed payment** splits the order total across two or more methods/terms. Each mixed payment line has its own rules:

<DocTable
  headers={['Line field', 'Rule']}
  rows={[
    ['Payment Method', 'Required — select the method for that installment.'],
    ['Payment Term', 'Required — select the term for that installment.'],
    ['Payment Value', 'Required and greater than zero.'],
    ['Value x Remaining', 'The installment value cannot be greater than the Remaining Amount (what is still left to pay of the total).'],
  ]}
/>

<Callout type="atencao" title="The sum must add up">
In mixed payment, the system controls the **Remaining Amount**. Each installment consumes part of the total; none can exceed what is left. Close the total value by distributing it across the methods.
</Callout>

## Link to the approval

The payment method influences the approval route: orders with **PIX or card** trigger the **Collection** step (financial check). The exact step and order come from the **Approval Rule** — see the <em>Order Approval - Without Ignition</em> flow.

## What this stretch does not do

- It does not register payment methods/terms — that is done in the Policies module.
- It does not process the transaction — it only defines the method and rules in the order.
