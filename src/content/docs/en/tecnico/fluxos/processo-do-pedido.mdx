## Overview

This is the **map of the sales order creation process** in S.I.N — the backbone that connects customer, items, payment, policies and the SAP integration. It serves both the **user** (to understand the end-to-end path) and **support** (to know where each rule, permission and parameter applies).

The order is built on top of a **customer**. It is the customer that defines the **Person Type** (Individual, Company or Foreign) and, from there, whether the order is **Domestic or International** and which **currency** applies. On top of that base come the **items**, the **payment method** and the **purchase policies**; when submitted, the order goes through **review/approval** and, once released, is **integrated into SAP** and moves on to **invoicing and picking**.

<Callout type="info" title="This is Part 1 (the map)">
This page is the overview. The detail of each stretch is in the series parts (by person type, items, payment, policies, permissions and parameters) — listed at the end. The **approval** path itself is in the <em>Order Approval - Without Ignition</em> flow.
</Callout>

<Callout type="atencao" title="Rules by item and by policy">
Beyond the fields above, submission validates the **items**: the product must exist, there can be no **product + item type duplicate**, **bonus** items only pass with an **allowed Smart Operation**, and **campaign** items must match the value required by the policy. The detail is in Parts 3 and 5.
</Callout>
