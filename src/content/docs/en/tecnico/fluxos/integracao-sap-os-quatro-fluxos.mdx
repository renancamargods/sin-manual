## Overview

This page details, step by step, the **four flows** the SAP integration chains: **Business Partner** (customer), **Sales Order** (order), **Outbound Delivery** (delivery) and **Billing Document** (invoice). The completion of one flow triggers the next, forming the chain **customer → order → delivery → invoice**.

For the engine mechanics, states and monitoring, see the [SAP Integration Overview](/en/tecnico/fluxos/integracao-sap-visao-geral).

## 1. Business Partner — Customer → SAP

Sends the customer to SAP as a **Business Partner**.

- **Start:** triggered for a specific customer (by the customer integration action).
- **Edge validation:** before any send, it checks **name**, **valid document** and **at least one address**. CNPJ is validated by format (accepts the alphanumeric CNPJ); CPF, by check digit.
- **Decision (the rule picks the path):**

/>

- **On completion:** it records, on the CRM customer, the **partner code** returned by SAP — that is what makes it "synced" for the next times.

<Callout type="info" title="Customer idempotency">
If the customer already has an SAP code, the integration preserves that code on reprocessing — it does not create a duplicate partner.
</Callout>

## 2. Sales Order — Order → SAP

Creates the order's **sales order** in SAP.

- **Start:** from an approved order.
- **Execution:** creates the sales order (Create) and captures the **SAP order number**.
- **On completion:**
  - records the **SAP order number** and the **sales order** in the CRM;
  - **chains** the **Outbound Delivery** (delivery) flow.
- **If it cannot identify the number** of the created order, the integration goes to **manual action** (the order exists in SAP but needs reconciliation).

## 3. Outbound Delivery — Delivery (3 steps)

Creates and processes the **delivery** for the sales order. The three steps are created together, but only the first starts **pending**; the others stay **"awaiting previous step"** and are released as each one completes.

<DocTable

When posting the goods issue (step 3), the integration **chains** the **Billing Document** (invoice) flow.

## 4. Billing Document — Invoice (by polling)

Obtains the **invoice**, which SAP generates **asynchronously**.

- **Start:** triggered by the completion of "post goods issue" (PostGoodsIssue).
- **Execution:** since there is no creation, the step **queries SAP repeatedly** (polling) filtering by the reference document.
- **Wait:** while the invoice does not appear, the step reschedules periodically, up to a maximum time; if it exceeds the limit, it escalates to **manual action**.

## Common problems

- **Sales chain doesn't move:** confirm that the **order** flow was triggered — delivery and invoice only exist chained from it.
- **Integration in "Awaiting manual action":** check, in monitoring, whether a required CRM field was missing or the SAP document number was not identified.
- **Invoice doesn't arrive:** SAP generates the invoice asynchronously; the invoice step stays in polling until it finds it or exceeds the limit.
- **Customer won't sync:** check name, valid document and at least one address — required before any send.
