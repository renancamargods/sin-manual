## Overview

**Quality Analysis** triages the products that **return** to stock, **classifies** each item as **sellable** or **scrap**, and **transfers** the result to the destination systems — **SAP** and/or **Delage** (logistics operator). **Scrap** holds the rules that flag a product/batch for attention or scrapping. Together, they ensure only fit items go back to stock and the rest is discarded with traceability.

## When to use this flow

- Triage and **classify** products that return to stock.
- **Transfer** the items to SAP and/or Delage according to the destination.
- Apply the **scrap rules** of a product/batch.

## Step 1 — New triage and product entry

In **"New Triage"**, do the **product entry** by scanning each item. Per item, it records **code**, **description**, **serial**, **batch**, **expiry date** and the **product address**. The system shows the number of **processed products** (items read).

## Step 3 — Transfer (SAP / Delage)

Once analyzed, the items are **transferred** to the destination (`TipoDestinoTransferencia`): **SAP** (0) and/or **Delage** (1). Each destination has its own status.

**SAP transfer (`StatusTransferenciaSap`)**

  headers={['Value', 'Status']}
  rows={[
    ['0', 'Pending'],
    ['1', 'In processing'],
    ['2', 'Success'],
    ['3', 'Failure'],
    ['4', 'Error (exception)'],
    ['5', 'Confirmed manually'],
    ['6', 'Rollback executed'],
  ]}
/>

**Delage transfer (`StatusTransferenciaDelage`)**

<DocTable
  headers={['Value', 'Status']}
  rows={[
    ['0', 'Not applicable'],
    ['1', 'Pending'],
    ['2', 'In processing'],
    ['3', 'Success'],
    ['4', 'Failure'],
    ['5', 'Error (exception)'],

/>

Actions available on failure or pending: **"Confirm SAP Manually"**, **"Resend Products"** (SAP) / **"Resend Products to Delage"**, **"Reset to Pending"** and **"SAP Rollback"** (with the confirmation **"Confirm the SAP Rollback?"**). The **"Transfer History"** and the **"Transfer Log"** record each attempt.

<Callout type="atencao" title="When the transfer cannot happen">
The system reports why it cannot transfer (`MotivoNaoPodeTransferir`): **a transfer is already in processing**, **all items were already transferred**, **no pending product**, or **analysis not found**.
</Callout>

## Relationship with other processes

- **PFCQ Occurrence:** quality analysis complements the handling of complaints/non-conformities — see [PFCQ Occurrence](/en/tecnico/fluxos/ocorrencia-pfcq).
- **Reverse Transfer:** the return of items between Lounges is in [Reverse Transfer](/en/tecnico/fluxos/transferencia-reversa).
- **Value Mapping:** `StatusAnaliseDeQualidade`, `StatusRefugo`, `TipoDestinoTransferencia`, `StatusTransferenciaSap`, `StatusTransferenciaDelage` and `MotivoNaoPodeTransferir` are in [Database → Value Mapping](/en/tecnico/banco-de-dados/de-para).

## Common problems

- **"There are products with SAP transfer failure":** use "Resend Products", "Confirm SAP Manually" or "SAP Rollback" as appropriate.
- **"There are products with failure sending to Delage":** use "Resend Products to Delage".
- **Cannot transfer:** check the reason (already in processing, all already transferred, none pending, or analysis not found).
- **Transfer stuck "In processing":** follow the history/log; if needed, "Reset to Pending".
