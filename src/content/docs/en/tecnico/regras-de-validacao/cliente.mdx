## Overview

Rules the **Customer** registration requires to save/submit, derived from the system validator. Many depend on the **person type** (Individual, Company or Foreign) and on the **country** (Portugal customers follow their own rules).

<Callout type="info" title="How to read">
"Always" = applies to all. "Individual/Company (domestic)" = Individual and Company in Brazil. "Company only", "Foreign only" and "Portugal" apply only in those cases.
</Callout>

## Portugal

For customers of the **Portugal** company, several domestic rules (Trade Name, CRO, State Registration, Birth Date, etc.) **do not apply**, and these become required: **Show VAT**, **Portugal Hub Customer** and **a primary Portugal address**.
