## Overview

Rules the **Order** requires to save/submit, derived from the system validator. Some depend on the **payment method/term**, on the **international customer** and on system **parameters**.

## Required — always

  headers={['Field', 'Rule']}

    ['Carrier', 'Required.'],
    ['Freight Type', 'Required.'],
    ['Priority', 'Required.'],

## Commercial Structure (required)

The whole commercial structure is required — **FVE**: Directorate, GNV, GR, GD and Consultant; **FVI**: Directorate, GNV, Coordinator, Supervisor and Consultant.

<Callout type="info" title="Out of policy = approval, not an error">
A valid order may still **not** be auto-approved: applying a **manual discount** on an item without policy/contract pushes the order **out of policy**, which requires approval. That is an approval flow, not a validation error — see [Order Approval - Without Ignition](/en/tecnico/fluxos/aprovacao-de-pedidos-sem-ignition) and the [Credit Engine](/en/tecnico/fluxos/motor-de-credito).
</Callout>
