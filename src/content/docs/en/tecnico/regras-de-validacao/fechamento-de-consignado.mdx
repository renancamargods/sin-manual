## Overview

Rules the **Consignment Closing** requires, derived from the system validators. They cover the **request** (generate the closing order) and the **discount** (on the item, on the equalization and on the policy).

<Callout type="info" title="Before generating">
The **"Search"** button (which loads the consignment items) only enables with **Customer** and **Invoice Number** filled in.
</Callout>
