## Overview

Rules the **Contract** requires to save, derived from the system validator. Some apply only to a **Course contract** or when the **ZapSign integration** is enabled.

## Required

<DocTable
  headers={['Field', 'Rule']}
  rows={[
    ['Title', 'Required; up to 150 characters.'],
    ['Signed', 'Required (Yes/No).'],
    ['Partner Type', 'Required.'],
    ['Contract Type', 'Required.'],
    ['Classification', 'Required.'],
    ['Has Partners', 'Required.'],
    ['Bonus Burs (Fresas Bonificadas)', 'Required.'],
    ['Status', 'Required.'],
    ['Customer', 'Required.'],
    ['Months of Validity', 'Required and greater than 0.'],
  ]}
/>

## Commercial Structure (required)

The whole structure is required: **Director**, **GNV**, **GR**, **GD** and **Consultant**.

## Validity, rebate and note

<DocTable
  headers={['Field', 'Rule']}
  rows={[
    ['Validity End', 'Must be later than the Validity Start.'],
    ['% Rebate', 'Between 0 and 100.'],
    ['Note', 'Up to 4000 characters.'],
  ]}
/>
