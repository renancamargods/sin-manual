## Overview

This page is a **map** of the flows documented under Technical Docs → Flows: it shows **how the processes connect**, from the first contact with the customer to invoicing and after-sales. Use it to get your bearings and jump straight to the flow you need.

## The sales chain, end to end

- **Lead → Customer:** a won [lead](/en/tecnico/fluxos/leads-pipeline-e-conversao) becomes a customer, who goes through [registration and approval](/en/tecnico/fluxos/criacao-de-cliente) with [credit/anti-fraud analysis](/en/tecnico/fluxos/credito-e-antifraude-clearsale).
- **Customer → Order:** the released customer places the [order](/en/tecnico/fluxos/processo-do-pedido), which is approved and routed to [payment](/en/tecnico/fluxos/pagamentos-geracao-de-link).
- **Order → SAP → Invoicing:** once approved and paid, the order proceeds to the [SAP integration](/en/tecnico/fluxos/integracao-sap-visao-geral) and [invoicing with serial movement](/en/tecnico/fluxos/faturamento-e-movimento-serial).
- **After-sales:** the invoiced item enables [exchange/return (TDC)](/en/tecnico/fluxos/troca-devolucao-cancelamento-tdc), [consignment](/en/tecnico/fluxos/fechamento-de-consignado) and [reverse transfer](/en/tecnico/fluxos/transferencia-reversa).
