## Overview

This page brings together an overview of how the main processes of the S.I.N Implant System connect across modules — for example, how a lead becomes a customer, how an order moves through to invoicing, or how an occurrence is opened and handled.

## When to use this page

- Understand the general sequence of a process that spans more than one module.
- Guide the configuration of rules that depend on another module.

<Callout type="info" title="Supporting content">
This section is aimed at teams responsible for system configuration, support and integration.
