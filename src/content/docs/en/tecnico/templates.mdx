## Overview

The system uses **HTML templates** to standardize the **emails, notifications and documents** it sends — such as the payment link, the occurrence notices and the bulk-import returns. Keeping this in templates ensures a visual standard and reduces rework.

<Callout type="info" title="Where they live">
The templates live in the system's services (REST Services, Integration Services, Worker and the Blazor app). Some templates are repeated across services — it is the **same template** used by different processes.
</Callout>

## User-configurable messages

Besides the HTML templates above (which live in the services' code), there are texts **configurable through the screens** of the Administrator module:

- **Custom Message** — standardized texts by **Message Type**.

<Callout type="cuidado" title="Adjust with the responsible area">
Changing a template affects **all sends** that use it. Adjustments should be made together with the area responsible for communication/marketing and tested before publishing.
</Callout>
